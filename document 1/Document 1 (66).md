# **2\. Purpose**

The Enterprise Partner Relationship Management (PRM) Platform enables Tamil Business Tribe (TBT) to establish, manage, grow, and optimize strategic business partnerships across multiple partner ecosystems.

The platform shall support partner onboarding, channel sales, affiliate programs, strategic alliances, reseller management, distributor operations, referral partnerships, co-marketing initiatives, revenue sharing, incentive management, certification, and partner success.

Rather than functioning as a traditional partner database, the PRM platform shall become a comprehensive Partner Ecosystem Operating System (PEOS) for managing global partner relationships.

---

# **3\. Vision**

The vision is to build an intelligent, AI-powered Partner Ecosystem that enables TBT to expand globally through trusted partners while maintaining governance, transparency, operational excellence, and measurable partner success.

The platform shall help TBT:

* Scale revenue through partnerships.  
* Expand into new markets.  
* Increase partner productivity.  
* Improve channel efficiency.  
* Strengthen strategic alliances.  
* Increase partner retention.  
* Improve customer acquisition.  
* Support ecosystem innovation.  
* Improve revenue predictability.  
* Enable collaborative business growth.

# **7\. Out of Scope**

The initial platform shall not:

* Replace ERP systems.  
* Replace Accounting Platforms.  
* Execute direct banking transactions.  
* Replace Legal Contract Management Systems.  
* Automatically approve commercial agreements.  
* Override enterprise compliance processes.  
* Replace external tax management systems.
