# **1\. Purpose**

The Enterprise Personalization Engine shall provide Tamil Business Tribe with a centralized intelligence system for delivering individualized customer experiences across the website, mobile application, community, courses, ebooks, podcasts, events, notifications, campaigns, membership services and commerce modules.

The system shall analyze each customer's profile, preferences, interests, behavior, engagement history, purchases, learning activity, community participation, current context and predicted intent.

Based on this intelligence, the platform shall automatically determine:

* What content should be displayed.  
* Which product should be recommended.  
* Which course should be suggested.  
* Which communication channel should be used.  
* Which offer should be presented.  
* What action the business should take next.  
* When the interaction should occur.  
* Which experience should be avoided.

The platform shall move TBT from basic rule-based personalization to real-time, AI-driven, one-to-one customer experiences.

---

# **2\. Product Vision**

The vision is to create a unified decisioning platform that understands every TBT member individually and continuously selects the most relevant experience for that person.

The system shall make every customer interaction feel:

* Relevant.  
* Contextual.  
* Timely.  
* Helpful.  
* Consistent.  
* Personalized.  
* Non-repetitive.  
* Privacy-aware.

The platform shall support both assisted personalization, where administrators define rules, and autonomous personalization, where AI selects and optimizes experiences within approved business policies.

# **7\. Personalization Levels**

The platform shall support multiple levels of personalization.

## **Level 1 – Basic Personalization**

Examples:

* Customer name.  
* Preferred language.  
* Location.  
* Membership type.  
* Device type.

## **Level 2 – Segment-Based Personalization**

Experiences shall be selected using:

* Lifecycle stage.  
* Customer value.  
* Engagement level.  
* Learning interest.  
* Business category.  
* Purchase history.

## **Level 3 – Behavioral Personalization**

Experiences shall respond to:

* Recent views.  
* Searches.  
* Clicks.  
* Course progress.  
* Podcast listening.  
* Ebook reading.  
* Community interactions.  
* Abandoned activities.

## **Level 4 – Predictive Personalization**

The system shall predict:

* Purchase intent.  
* Content interest.  
* Membership upgrade probability.  
* Churn risk.  
* Preferred engagement time.  
* Preferred communication channel.

## **Level 5 – Real-Time Individual Personalization**

The system shall make a unique decision for each customer using current context, predictive models and business policies.

# **11\. Recommendation Methods**

The platform shall support:

## **Content-Based Recommendations**

Recommend items similar to content previously consumed by the customer.

## **Collaborative Filtering**

Recommend items consumed or purchased by customers with similar behavior.

## **Popularity-Based Recommendations**

Recommend trending or frequently consumed items.

## **Segment-Based Recommendations**

Recommend items based on audience segment characteristics.

## **Contextual Recommendations**

Recommend items based on current screen, time, location or session activity.

## **Predictive Recommendations**

Use machine learning to predict the item most likely to produce a desired outcome.

## **Hybrid Recommendations**

Combine multiple methods to improve relevance and reliability.

## **Risk: AI Bias**

**Mitigation:** Bias testing, sensitive-feature restrictions and human review.

## **Risk: Privacy Violation**

**Mitigation:** Consent enforcement, data minimization and audit logging.

## **Risk: Repetitive Recommendations**

**Mitigation:** Exposure limits, content diversity and cooldown rules.

## **Risk: Model Failure**

**Mitigation:** Rule-based fallback and default experiences.

## **Risk: Customer Confusion**

**Mitigation:** Stable navigation, transparent explanations and controlled UI variation.

## **Risk: Excessive Operating Cost**

**Mitigation:** Caching, model-tier selection, batch processing and cost monitoring.
