# **24\. Customer Health Scoring**

## **Purpose**

The Customer Health Scoring Platform enables Tamil Business Tribe (TBT) to continuously evaluate the overall health, engagement, satisfaction, adoption, and business value of every customer.

The platform shall calculate dynamic health scores using configurable business rules, operational metrics, behavioral analytics, commercial performance, and Artificial Intelligence.

Customer Health Scoring shall become the primary indicator for proactive customer success management.

---

## **Business Objectives**

The platform shall enable TBT to:

* Identify healthy customers.  
* Detect at-risk customers early.  
* Improve customer retention.  
* Prioritize Customer Success activities.  
* Increase customer satisfaction.  
* Improve renewal rates.  
* Reduce churn.  
* Increase expansion opportunities.  
* Support executive decision-making.  
* Enable AI-driven customer success.

---

## **Health Score Components**

The health score shall be calculated using configurable weighted metrics including:

* Product Usage  
* Feature Adoption  
* Login Frequency  
* Customer Engagement  
* Learning Completion  
* Support Activity  
* Resolution Time  
* Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)  
* Net Promoter Score (NPS)  
* Renewal History  
* Revenue Contribution  
* Executive Participation  
* Community Activity  
* Payment Status  
* Expansion Potential

Organizations shall configure metric weightages.

---

## **Health Categories**

Customers shall automatically be classified as:

* Excellent  
* Healthy  
* Stable  
* Watch List  
* At Risk  
* Critical  
* Recovery Required

Thresholds shall remain configurable.

---

## **Health Monitoring**

The platform shall continuously monitor:

* Daily Health Changes  
* Weekly Trends  
* Monthly Trends  
* Product Usage Decline  
* Engagement Changes  
* Satisfaction Changes  
* Revenue Risk  
* Executive Escalations  
* Success Activities  
* AI Risk Signals

---

## **AI Health Intelligence**

Artificial Intelligence shall provide:

* Health Forecasts  
* Root Cause Analysis  
* Improvement Recommendations  
* Health Trend Predictions  
* Success Plan Suggestions  
* Risk Prioritization

Every AI recommendation shall include confidence scores, reasoning, and supporting evidence.

# **25\. Churn Prediction Management**

## **Purpose**

The Churn Prediction Management Platform enables proactive identification of customers who are likely to discontinue using TBT products or services.

The platform shall combine operational metrics, customer behavior, commercial indicators, and AI models to predict churn before it occurs.

---

## **Churn Indicators**

The platform shall monitor:

* Reduced Product Usage  
* Login Decline  
* Feature Abandonment  
* Support Escalations  
* Negative Feedback  
* Low CSAT  
* Declining NPS  
* Payment Delays  
* Missed Business Reviews  
* Reduced Community Participation  
* Executive Relationship Gaps  
* Renewal Delays

---

## **Churn Risk Levels**

Customers shall be classified as:

* Very Low Risk  
* Low Risk  
* Medium Risk  
* High Risk  
* Critical Risk

Organizations may configure additional risk levels.

---

## **Churn Prevention Playbooks**

The platform shall support:

* Customer Outreach  
* Executive Engagement  
* Product Training  
* Dedicated Support  
* Success Planning  
* Commercial Reviews  
* Feature Adoption Campaigns  
* Renewal Acceleration  
* Recovery Programs

---

## **AI Churn Intelligence**

Artificial Intelligence shall generate:

* Churn Probability  
* Root Cause Analysis  
* Customer Recovery Strategies  
* Revenue Impact Predictions  
* Recommended Success Actions  
* Escalation Priorities

AI outputs shall remain advisory and auditable.

---

# **26\. Renewal Management**

## **Purpose**

The Renewal Management Platform enables structured planning, forecasting, execution, and optimization of customer contract renewals.

The platform shall reduce renewal risk while increasing long-term customer retention.

---

## **Renewal Lifecycle**

Every renewal shall progress through:

1. Renewal Identification  
2. Success Review  
3. Commercial Assessment  
4. Executive Engagement  
5. Proposal Preparation  
6. Negotiation  
7. Approval  
8. Contract Renewal  
9. Revenue Recognition

Each stage shall support configurable workflows.

---

## **Renewal Components**

The platform shall manage:

* Contract Information  
* Renewal Timeline  
* Renewal Forecast  
* Commercial Terms  
* Customer Health  
* Executive Sponsors  
* Renewal Risks  
* Expansion Opportunities  
* Approval Workflow  
* Audit History

---

## **Renewal Automation**

The platform shall automatically:

* Generate renewal reminders.  
* Notify stakeholders.  
* Schedule executive reviews.  
* Launch renewal playbooks.  
* Escalate overdue renewals.  
* Predict renewal probability.  
* Recommend pricing strategies.

---

## **AI Renewal Intelligence**

Artificial Intelligence shall provide:

* Renewal Probability  
* Commercial Recommendations  
* Discount Optimization  
* Executive Engagement Suggestions  
* Contract Risk Analysis  
* Revenue Forecasts

## **Advocacy Programs**

The platform shall support:

* Reference Customers  
* Case Studies  
* Testimonials  
* Product Reviews  
* Success Stories  
* Referral Programs

* Community Champions

* Advisory Boards

* Refer new customers.

* Community Leadership Opportunities

# **29\. Customer Success Intelligence Dashboard**

## **Purpose**

The Customer Success Intelligence Dashboard provides executives with a unified, real-time view of customer health, churn risk, renewals, expansion opportunities, advocacy performance, and strategic customer success initiatives.

The dashboard shall consolidate operational, commercial, behavioral, financial, and AI-generated intelligence into a comprehensive executive workspace.

---

## **Executive Dashboard Components**

The dashboard shall include:

* Total Customers  
* Customer Health Distribution  
* Churn Risk Overview  
* Active Renewals  
* Renewal Forecast  
* Expansion Pipeline  
* Expansion Revenue  
* Advocacy Participation  
* Customer Satisfaction  
* Net Promoter Score (NPS)  
* Executive Alerts  
* AI Customer Rankings

---

## **AI Customer Success Intelligence**

Artificial Intelligence shall provide:

* Health Predictions  
* Churn Forecasts  
* Renewal Probability  
* Expansion Recommendations  
* Advocacy Opportunities  
* Customer Lifetime Value Forecasts  
* Revenue Predictions  
* Executive Strategic Insights

Every AI-generated insight shall be transparent, configurable, explainable, and fully auditable.

---

## **Executive Reporting**

The platform shall generate configurable reports including:

* Customer Health Report  
* Churn Risk Report  
* Renewal Performance Report  
* Expansion Revenue Report  
* Advocacy Performance Report  
* Customer Lifetime Value Report  
* Executive Success Review  
* AI Customer Success Intelligence Report  
* Quarterly Customer Success Business Review  
* Annual Customer Success Performance Report

Reports shall support scheduled delivery, PDF/Excel export, drill-down analytics, historical comparisons, predictive forecasting, benchmarking, role-based access control, and enterprise data retention policies.
