# **11\. Enterprise Revenue Management**

## **Purpose**

The Enterprise Revenue Management Platform enables Tamil Business Tribe (TBT) to centrally manage every revenue-generating activity across the complete customer lifecycle, from demand generation and opportunity creation to customer acquisition, renewals, expansion, partner revenue, and long-term revenue optimization.

The platform shall provide a unified Revenue 360° Workspace that consolidates commercial, operational, financial, customer, partner, and AI-generated intelligence into a single enterprise view.

Revenue Management shall become the authoritative source of truth for all revenue-related information across the TBT ecosystem.

---

## **Business Objectives**

The platform shall enable TBT to:

* Centralize revenue operations.  
* Improve revenue visibility.  
* Increase Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR).  
* Improve Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR).  
* Reduce revenue leakage.  
* Increase customer lifetime value.  
* Improve commercial execution.  
* Strengthen executive decision-making.  
* Improve revenue predictability.  
* Support enterprise-scale revenue growth.

---

## **Revenue Categories**

The platform shall support configurable revenue classifications including:

* Subscription Revenue  
* Membership Revenue  
* Course Revenue  
* Digital Product Revenue  
* Service Revenue  
* Consulting Revenue  
* Marketplace Revenue  
* Affiliate Revenue  
* Partner Revenue  
* Advertising Revenue  
* Event Revenue  
* Enterprise Contract Revenue  
* Renewal Revenue  
* Expansion Revenue  
* Professional Services Revenue

Organizations may configure additional revenue categories without software modifications.

---

## **Revenue Profile**

Each revenue record shall maintain:

* Revenue ID  
* Customer ID  
* Opportunity ID  
* Product or Service  
* Revenue Category  
* Sales Channel  
* Subscription Plan  
* Revenue Owner  
* Revenue Source  
* Contract Value  
* Billing Frequency  
* Currency  
* Recognition Schedule  
* Renewal Date  
* Expansion Potential  
* AI Revenue Score

---

## **Revenue 360° Workspace**

The unified revenue workspace shall include:

* Revenue Overview  
* Customer Information  
* Commercial Timeline  
* Product Portfolio  
* Revenue Recognition  
* Billing History  
* Renewal Schedule  
* Expansion Opportunities  
* Pipeline Status  
* Forecast History  
* Performance KPIs  
* Executive Notes  
* AI Recommendations  
* Audit History

# **13\. Revenue Pipeline Management**

## **Purpose**

The Revenue Pipeline Management Platform enables continuous monitoring, optimization, and governance of all commercial opportunities from lead qualification through revenue realization.

The platform shall ensure predictable pipeline health and maximize conversion efficiency.

---

## **Pipeline Stages**

The platform shall support:

1. Lead Identified  
2. Qualified Lead  
3. Discovery  
4. Opportunity Created  
5. Proposal  
6. Negotiation  
7. Contract Approval  
8. Closed Won  
9. Revenue Recognition

Pipeline stages shall remain configurable.

---

## **Pipeline Metrics**

The platform shall monitor:

* Pipeline Value  
* Qualified Pipeline  
* Weighted Pipeline  
* Pipeline Coverage Ratio  
* Win Rate  
* Average Deal Size  
* Sales Cycle Length  
* Pipeline Velocity  
* Conversion Rate  
* Revenue Realization

---

## **Pipeline Automation**

The platform shall automatically:

* Identify stalled opportunities.  
* Recommend next actions.  
* Notify revenue owners.  
* Trigger executive escalations.  
* Launch follow-up workflows.  
* Predict close probability.  
* Update revenue forecasts.

Automation rules shall remain configurable.

---

## **AI Pipeline Intelligence**

Artificial Intelligence shall provide:

* Pipeline Risk Analysis  
* Deal Prioritization  
* Revenue Probability  
* Sales Recommendations  
* Opportunity Health Scores  
* Commercial Forecasting

All AI-generated insights shall remain advisory.
