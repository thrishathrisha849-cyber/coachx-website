# **3\. AI PRODUCT PRINCIPLES**

## **3.1 AI as an Assistant, Not an Authority**

TBT AI user-kku support provide pannum.

TBT AI:

* Final legal authority illa.  
* Financial advisor illa.  
* Medical professional illa.  
* Guaranteed business expert illa.  
* Human mentor replacement illa.  
* User business result guarantee panna koodathu.

## **3.2 User-Controlled Output**

AI output:

* Edit panna mudiyanum.  
* Copy panna mudiyanum.  
* Save panna mudiyanum.  
* Regenerate panna mudiyanum.  
* Shorten, expand or translate panna mudiyanum.  
* Different tone-la rewrite panna mudiyanum.  
* Delete panna mudiyanum.

## **3.3 Ask Only Necessary Questions**

User request sufficient-aa irundha immediate output generate pannanum.

Material details missing-na only high-value clarification request pannanum.

Minor details missing-na reasonable placeholders or clearly marked assumptions use pannalaam.

## **3.4 No Silent Fabrication**

AI uncertain facts-ai confirmed fact madhiri present panna koodathu.

Required behaviors:

* Assumption label  
* Uncertainty statement  
* Verification suggestion  
* Source citation when platform retrieval used  
* User confirmation before high-impact action

## **3.5 Human Review for High-Impact Output**

Recommended human review:

* Legal agreements  
* Tax filings  
* Financial projections  
* Investment decisions  
* Employment decisions  
* Public crisis communication  
* Medical or mental-health guidance  
* High-value advertisements  
* Contractual promises  
* Regulatory submissions

## **3.6 Privacy by Design**

User conversations and files:

* Public content-aa default treat panna koodathu.  
* Other members-ku reveal panna koodathu.  
* Model training-ku use pannurathu explicit policy and consent illama nadakka koodathu.  
* Minimum necessary retention follow pannanum.

## **3.7 Provider Independence**

Business logic model-provider-specific SDK-la tightly couple panna koodathu.

AI gateway provider switching, fallback and model routing support pannanum.
