# **33\. Enterprise Account Management**

## **Purpose**

The Enterprise Account Management Platform enables Tamil Business Tribe (TBT) to manage every customer account throughout its complete business relationship, from initial conversion through expansion, renewal, advocacy, and long-term strategic growth.

The platform shall provide a unified account workspace that consolidates commercial, operational, financial, customer success, marketing, and support information into a single enterprise view.

Account Management shall become the central repository for all business relationships across the TBT ecosystem.

---

## **Business Objectives**

The platform shall enable TBT to:

* Centralize customer account information.  
* Improve account visibility.  
* Increase customer lifetime value (CLV).  
* Improve cross-functional collaboration.  
* Increase account expansion.  
* Improve customer retention.  
* Strengthen executive relationships.  
* Support enterprise account planning.  
* Improve renewal performance.  
* Maximize long-term revenue growth.

---

## **Account Categories**

The platform shall support configurable account classifications including:

* Individual Customer  
* Small Business  
* Medium Business  
* Enterprise Customer  
* Government Organization  
* Educational Institution  
* Startup  
* Strategic Partner  
* Channel Partner  
* Corporate Group  
* International Customer  
* Key Account

Organizations may define additional account categories.

---

## **Account Profile**

Each account shall maintain:

* Account ID  
* Account Name  
* Account Type  
* Industry  
* Company Size  
* Annual Revenue  
* Employee Count  
* Headquarters Location  
* Billing Information  
* Subscription Details  
* Customer Success Owner  
* Sales Owner  
* Executive Sponsor  
* Risk Rating  
* Health Score  
* Lifetime Revenue  
* Renewal Date  
* Expansion Opportunities  
* AI Account Score

---

## **Account Workspace**

The unified account workspace shall include:

* Contacts  
* Opportunities  
* Active Contracts  
* Invoices  
* Support Cases  
* Customer Success Plans  
* Community Participation  
* Learning Progress  
* AI Insights  
* Executive Notes

* Approval History

# **40\. Enterprise Acceptance Criteria**

## **Sales Operations**

* Every lead shall progress through configurable qualification workflows.  
* Opportunities shall maintain complete lifecycle traceability.  
* Pipelines shall support configurable stages and health monitoring.  
* Sales activities shall be linked to opportunities and accounts.

---

## **Revenue Management**

* Revenue forecasts shall support multiple configurable forecasting models.  
* Revenue intelligence shall aggregate data from integrated enterprise systems.  
* Revenue analytics shall support real-time and historical reporting.  
* Forecast calculations shall maintain version history and auditability.

---

## **Account & Contact Management**

* Every customer account shall maintain a unified enterprise profile.  
* Contacts shall support relationship mapping and stakeholder analysis.  
* Strategic account plans shall support executive collaboration.  
* Account health scores shall update automatically based on configurable rules.

---

## **AI Intelligence**

* AI shall provide lead, opportunity, pipeline, account, and revenue insights.  
* AI-generated recommendations shall include confidence scores.  
* AI predictions shall remain transparent, explainable, and reviewable.  
* AI recommendations shall never automatically modify enterprise data without approved workflows.

---

## **Governance & Compliance**

* Every commercial transaction shall maintain immutable audit logs.  
* Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) shall govern access to revenue data.  
* Sensitive commercial information shall be encrypted in transit and at rest.  
* Sales governance workflows shall support configurable approvals and policy enforcement.

---

## **Performance & Scalability**

* The platform shall support millions of leads, accounts, contacts, and opportunities.  
* Revenue dashboards shall remain responsive under enterprise-scale workloads.  
* Analytics and AI processing shall operate independently from transactional workloads.  
* The architecture shall support future expansion into global sales organizations, partner ecosystems, and advanced revenue operations capabilities.
