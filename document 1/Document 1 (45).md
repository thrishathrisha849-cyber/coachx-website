# **2\. Purpose**

The Enterprise Competitive Intelligence, Market Research, Industry Benchmarking, Customer Research & Strategic Insights Platform enables Tamil Business Tribe to understand its market, customers, competitors, industry trends, and future business opportunities.

The platform shall collect, organize, analyze, and transform internal and external market information into actionable strategic intelligence.

This system shall help TBT make informed decisions related to:

* Product Development  
* Market Expansion  
* Customer Acquisition  
* Pricing  
* Membership Plans  
* Course Creation  
* Community Development  
* Brand Positioning  
* Campaign Planning  
* Strategic Partnerships  
* Business Growth

The platform shall reduce decisions based only on assumptions and replace them with measurable, evidence-based insights.

---

# **3\. Vision**

The vision is to create a centralized enterprise intelligence system that continuously monitors the business environment and helps Tamil Business Tribe identify:

* Market Changes  
* Customer Expectations  
* Competitor Activities  
* Industry Opportunities  
* Business Threats  
* Emerging Customer Needs  
* New Revenue Opportunities  
* Product Gaps  
* Strategic Growth Areas

The platform shall function as an enterprise decision-support system for TBT leadership and business teams.

---

# **4\. Business Objectives**

The platform shall enable TBT to:

* Understand the current market environment.  
* Monitor direct and indirect competitors.  
* Identify competitor strengths and weaknesses.  
* Discover unmet customer needs.  
* Compare TBT performance with industry benchmarks.  
* Identify emerging industry trends.  
* Improve product and service positioning.  
* Support evidence-based strategic planning.  
* Discover new market opportunities.  
* Reduce competitive risks.  
* Improve pricing and packaging decisions.  
* Understand different customer segments.  
* Improve future product roadmaps.  
* Create accurate strategic reports.

---

# **5\. Core Platform Principles**

The platform shall follow these enterprise principles:

## **5.1 Evidence-Based Intelligence**

All strategic insights should be connected to measurable data, research findings, customer evidence, or approved analyst observations.

## **5.2 Continuous Monitoring**

The system shall continuously monitor configured competitors, market categories, customer signals, and industry developments.

## **5.3 Centralized Intelligence**

All approved market research and competitive intelligence shall be maintained in a centralized repository.

## **5.4 Human and AI Collaboration**

AI may assist with collection, classification, summarization, comparison, forecasting, and recommendations.

Final strategic decisions shall remain under authorized human control.

## **5.5 Source Transparency**

Every external insight shall clearly display:

* Information Source  
* Collection Date  
* Last Updated Date  
* Confidence Level  
* Analyst Notes  
* Verification Status

## **5.6 Security and Confidentiality**

Sensitive research, internal strategies, competitor assessments, and future business plans shall only be accessible to authorized users.

## **5.7 Action-Oriented Insights**

The platform shall not only display raw information. It shall help teams understand:

* What changed  
* Why it matters  
* What the possible business impact is  
* What action should be considered  
* Who should own the next action

# **7\. Out of Scope**

The initial platform shall not:

* Perform unauthorized access to competitor systems.  
* Collect private competitor information illegally.  
* Bypass website security controls.  
* Purchase confidential data from unauthorized sources.  
* Automatically publish strategic reports publicly.  
* Make final business decisions without human approval.  
* Replace legal, financial, or professional market advisors.  
* Collect customer research data without required consent.

All competitive intelligence activities shall follow applicable laws, platform terms, privacy requirements, and TBT governance policies.

# **8\. Enterprise Intelligence Architecture**

The platform shall follow a multi-layer intelligence architecture.

## **Layer 1 – Intelligence Sources**

This layer receives information from approved internal and external sources.

Possible sources include:

* TBT Customer Feedback  
* CRM Data  
* Sales Data  
* Membership Data  
* Course Performance  
* Community Discussions  
* Customer Interviews  
* Market Surveys  
* Public Competitor Websites  
* Public Product Pages  
* Public Pricing Pages  
* Industry Reports  
* Research Publications  
* Public Reviews  
* News Sources  
* Social Media  
* Government Data  
* Partner Information  
* Analyst Notes

---

## **Layer 2 – Data Collection and Validation**

This layer shall:

* Capture research data.  
* Record the original source.  
* Validate required fields.  
* Detect duplicate information.  
* Assign collection timestamps.  
* Check source reliability.  
* Identify outdated information.  
* Route uncertain information for verification.

---

## **Layer 3 – Intelligence Processing**

This layer shall use approved analytical and AI capabilities to:

* Classify information.  
* Extract competitors.  
* Detect products and features.  
* Identify pricing changes.  
* Detect market trends.  
* Summarize research findings.  
* Compare competitors.  
* Detect opportunities and risks.  
* Generate preliminary recommendations.

---

## **Layer 4 – Strategic Analysis**

This layer shall transform processed information into:

* Competitor Comparisons  
* Market Opportunity Reports  
* Customer Research Findings  
* Industry Benchmark Reports  
* Strategic Risk Assessments  
* Positioning Recommendations  
* Product Gap Analysis  
* Growth Opportunities

---

## **Layer 5 – Business Action**

Approved insights may create:

* Product Roadmap Recommendations  
* Marketing Tasks  
* Pricing Reviews  
* Customer Research Projects  
* Campaign Opportunities  
* Executive Alerts  
* Strategy Review Tasks  
* Partnership Investigations

---

## **Layer 6 – Dashboards and Reporting**

Authorized users shall receive information through:

* Executive Dashboards  
* Competitor Dashboards  
* Market Research Reports  
* Benchmark Reports  
* Customer Insight Reports  
* Opportunity Dashboards  
* Risk Dashboards  
* Scheduled Intelligence Briefings

---

# **9\. Intelligence Lifecycle**

Every intelligence record shall follow a defined lifecycle:

1. Information Identified  
2. Source Recorded  
3. Information Collected  
4. Validation Performed  
5. Classification Completed  
6. Analyst Review Performed  
7. Insight Generated  
8. Business Impact Assessed  
9. Recommendation Created  
10. Decision Owner Assigned  
11. Action Tracked  
12. Outcome Measured  
13. Intelligence Archived or Updated

This lifecycle shall ensure that research findings are converted into measurable business action.
