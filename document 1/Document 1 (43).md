# **30\. Enterprise Experience Insights Platform**

## **Purpose**

The Experience Insights Platform converts customer feedback, behavioral data, and operational metrics into actionable business intelligence. Instead of showing isolated feedback records, the platform provides a complete understanding of the customer experience across the entire TBT ecosystem.

---

## **Objectives**

The platform shall:

* Measure customer experience continuously.  
* Identify customer pain points.  
* Detect successful experiences.  
* Measure journey quality.  
* Compare experiences across products.  
* Recommend experience improvements.  
* Support executive decision-making.

---

## **Experience Dimensions**

Customer experience shall be measured across:

* Registration Experience  
* Onboarding Experience  
* Learning Experience  
* Community Experience  
* Membership Experience  
* Event Experience  
* AI Assistant Experience  
* Support Experience  
* Renewal Experience  
* Overall Brand Experience

Each dimension shall have its own Experience Score and historical trend.

---

## **Experience Scorecard**

Every experience category shall display:

* Overall Score  
* Satisfaction Percentage  
* Average Rating  
* Positive Feedback Count  
* Negative Feedback Count  
* Completion Rate  
* Improvement Trend  
* Benchmark Score

---

# **31\. Enterprise Trend Analysis Engine**

## **Purpose**

The Trend Analysis Engine continuously monitors customer feedback over time to identify emerging opportunities and risks before they become major business issues.

---

## **Trend Types**

The engine shall detect:

* Daily Trends  
* Weekly Trends  
* Monthly Trends  
* Quarterly Trends  
* Annual Trends  
* Seasonal Trends  
* Product Trends  
* Customer Segment Trends  
* Geographic Trends

---

## **Trend Indicators**

For every trend, the system shall provide:

* Growth Percentage  
* Decline Percentage  
* Trend Direction  
* Confidence Level  
* Business Impact  
* Suggested Action

---

## **Trend Alerts**

Examples:

* Course satisfaction decreased by 12% this week.  
* Payment complaints increased by 28%.  
* Community engagement improved by 15%.  
* Event feedback reached a new monthly high.

Critical trends shall automatically notify administrators.

---

# **32\. Enterprise Root Cause Intelligence**

## **Purpose**

The Root Cause Intelligence Engine identifies the underlying reasons behind customer complaints instead of only reporting symptoms.

---

## **AI Investigation Process**

The AI shall:

1. Collect related feedback.  
2. Group similar issues.  
3. Detect recurring patterns.  
4. Identify affected features.  
5. Estimate business impact.  
6. Recommend probable root causes.  
7. Suggest corrective actions.

---

## **Example**

**Problem**

Customers report payment failures.

**Possible Root Cause**

* Payment gateway timeout.  
* Network latency.  
* Third-party API outage.  
* Invalid coupon validation.  
* Membership configuration error.

The platform shall rank each cause by probability and impact.

---

## **Root Cause Dashboard**

Each detected issue shall include:

* Issue Summary  
* Probable Root Cause  
* Confidence Score  
* Number of Affected Customers  
* Revenue Impact  
* Priority Level  
* Suggested Resolution  
* Responsible Team

---
