# **11\. Enterprise Lead Management**

## **Purpose**

The Enterprise Lead Management Platform enables Tamil Business Tribe (TBT) to acquire, organize, qualify, prioritize, track, and convert potential customers into qualified sales opportunities through an intelligent, automated, and scalable lead management process.

The platform shall provide a centralized lead repository integrated with marketing, customer success, AI intelligence, and executive analytics.

Lead Management shall become the foundation of the Enterprise Revenue Operating System (RevOS).

---

## **Business Objectives**

The platform shall enable TBT to:

* Capture high-quality leads.  
* Eliminate duplicate leads.  
* Improve lead qualification.  
* Increase lead conversion.  
* Reduce lead response time.  
* Improve sales productivity.  
* Support automated lead routing.  
* Improve marketing attribution.  
* Increase pipeline quality.  
* Maximize revenue opportunities.

---

## **Lead Categories**

The platform shall support configurable lead classifications including:

* Website Lead  
* Community Lead  
* Referral Lead  
* Event Lead  
* Webinar Lead  
* Email Campaign Lead  
* WhatsApp Lead  
* Organic Lead  
* Paid Campaign Lead  
* AI Assistant Lead  
* Partner Lead  
* Manual Lead  
* Enterprise Lead

Administrators shall be able to create additional lead categories without software modifications.

---

## **Lead Information**

Each lead record shall include:

* Lead ID  
* Full Name  
* Company Name  
* Job Title  
* Email Address  
* Phone Number  
* Country  
* Industry  
* Company Size  
* Revenue Range  
* Lead Source  
* Product Interest  
* Assigned Owner  
* Lead Status  
* Lead Score  
* Lifecycle Stage  
* Created Date  
* Last Activity  
* Communication Timeline  
* Consent Status

---

## **Lead Lifecycle**

Every lead shall progress through:

1. Captured  
2. Validated  
3. Enriched  
4. Qualified  
5. Assigned  
6. Contacted  
7. Engaged  
8. Sales Qualified  
9. Opportunity Created  
10. Converted  
11. Closed  
12. Archived

Each transition shall maintain a complete audit history.

---

# **13\. Enterprise Lead Qualification**

## **Purpose**

The Lead Qualification Platform ensures that only sales-ready leads are forwarded to the sales organization, improving efficiency and increasing conversion rates.

---

## **Qualification Framework**

The platform shall support configurable qualification models such as:

* BANT  
* MEDDICC  
* CHAMP  
* GPCT  
* Custom Enterprise Models

Organizations may define proprietary qualification frameworks.

---

## **Qualification Criteria**

The platform shall evaluate:

* Budget  
* Authority  
* Business Need  
* Timeline  
* Product Fit  
* Customer Intent  
* Company Size  
* Revenue Potential  
* Industry  
* Engagement Level  
* AI Confidence Score

---

## **Qualification Outcomes**

Leads shall be classified as:

* Marketing Qualified Lead (MQL)  
* Sales Accepted Lead (SAL)  
* Sales Qualified Lead (SQL)  
* Enterprise Qualified Lead  
* Nurture Required  
* Disqualified

---

## **AI Qualification Intelligence**

Artificial Intelligence shall:

* Recommend qualification status.  
* Detect buying signals.  
* Predict purchase intent.  
* Estimate deal value.  
* Identify hidden opportunities.  
* Recommend next actions.

All AI decisions shall remain reviewable.

# **15\. Enterprise Lead Assignment Management**

## **Purpose**

The Lead Assignment Platform automatically distributes qualified leads to the most appropriate sales representatives using configurable assignment strategies.

---

## **Assignment Methods**

Supported assignment methods include:

* Round Robin  
* Geographic Territory  
* Industry Expertise  
* Product Expertise  
* Enterprise Account Ownership  
* Capacity-Based Assignment  
* Performance-Based Assignment  
* AI Intelligent Assignment  
* Manual Assignment

---

## **Assignment Rules**

The platform shall consider:

* Representative Availability  
* Existing Customer Relationships  
* Language Preferences  
* Time Zone  
* Skill Level  
* Industry Experience  
* Workload Balance  
* Customer Priority

---

## **Assignment Workflow**

Each assignment shall include:

1. Qualification  
2. Assignment  
3. Notification  
4. Acceptance  
5. Follow-Up  
6. Activity Tracking  
7. Escalation (if overdue)
