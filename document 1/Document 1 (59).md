# **27\. Enterprise Customer Lifecycle Management**

## **Purpose**

The Enterprise Customer Lifecycle Management (CLM) Platform enables Tamil Business Tribe (TBT) to manage every customer relationship from first interaction through long-term advocacy, expansion, and re-engagement.

The platform shall continuously monitor customer progression across lifecycle stages, automatically trigger engagement strategies, identify risks, and maximize customer lifetime value (CLV).

Customer Lifecycle Management shall become a strategic capability that aligns marketing, sales, product, customer success, community, and support teams around a single customer journey.

---

## **Business Objectives**

The platform shall enable TBT to:

* Manage the complete customer lifecycle.  
* Improve lifecycle progression.  
* Increase customer activation.  
* Reduce lifecycle drop-offs.  
* Increase customer engagement.  
* Improve customer retention.  
* Increase renewal rates.  
* Maximize customer lifetime value.  
* Improve customer loyalty.  
* Increase advocacy and referrals.

---

## **Customer Lifecycle Stages**

The platform shall support configurable lifecycle stages including:

* Anonymous Visitor  
* Marketing Lead  
* Qualified Lead  
* Prospect  
* Registered User  
* Trial User  
* Paying Customer  
* Active Customer  
* Power User  
* Premium Member  
* Enterprise Customer  
* Advocate  
* Referral Partner  
* Dormant Customer  
* Churned Customer  
* Win-back Customer  
* Alumni

Each stage shall have configurable entry criteria, exit conditions, KPIs, automations, and ownership rules.

---

## **Lifecycle Automation**

The platform shall automatically:

* Detect lifecycle transitions.  
* Trigger onboarding workflows.  
* Assign Customer Success tasks.  
* Launch engagement campaigns.  
* Recommend products.  
* Schedule lifecycle reviews.  
* Notify responsible teams.  
* Escalate high-risk accounts.

All automation rules shall be configurable.

---

# **34\. Enterprise Customer Lifecycle Intelligence Dashboard**

## **Purpose**

The Customer Lifecycle Intelligence Dashboard provides executives with a centralized, real-time view of lifecycle progression, engagement, retention, advocacy, referrals, and customer value.

The dashboard shall combine operational, financial, behavioral, and AI-driven insights into a unified executive workspace.

---

The dashboard shall include:

* Lifecycle Stage Distribution  
* Customer Growth Trends  
* Customer Health Overview  
* Segmentation Analysis  
* Persona Performance  
* Engagement Trends  
* Loyalty Program Performance  
* Referral Performance  
* Advocacy Metrics  
* Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)  
* Churn Forecast  
* Renewal Forecast  
* Expansion Opportunities  
* AI Executive Recommendations

---

## **AI Lifecycle Intelligence**

Artificial Intelligence shall provide:

* Lifecycle Transition Predictions  
* Customer Value Forecasts  
* Retention Probability  
* Loyalty Growth Predictions  
* Referral Opportunity Detection  
* Expansion Opportunity Forecasts  
* Persona Evolution Analysis  
* Customer Journey Recommendations

## **Executive Reporting**
