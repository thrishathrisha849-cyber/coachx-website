# **11\. Enterprise Customer Management**

## **Purpose**

The Enterprise Customer Management Platform enables Tamil Business Tribe (TBT) to centrally manage every customer throughout the complete customer lifecycle, from registration through onboarding, adoption, expansion, renewal, advocacy, and long-term strategic engagement.

The platform shall provide a unified Customer 360° Workspace that consolidates commercial, operational, behavioral, financial, learning, community, support, and AI-generated intelligence into a single enterprise view.

Customer Management shall become the authoritative source of truth for all customer-related information across the TBT ecosystem.

---

## **Business Objectives**

The platform shall enable TBT to:

* Centralize customer information.  
* Improve customer visibility.  
* Increase customer lifetime value (CLV).  
* Improve customer engagement.  
* Reduce operational complexity.  
* Improve retention.  
* Strengthen customer relationships.  
* Improve executive visibility.  
* Enable personalized experiences.  
* Support enterprise-scale customer operations.

---

## **Customer Categories**

The platform shall support configurable customer classifications including:

* Individual Customer  
* Premium Customer  
* Enterprise Customer  
* Government Customer  
* Educational Institution  
* Startup Customer  
* Non-Profit Organization  
* Strategic Customer  
* International Customer  
* Community Member  
* Partner Customer  
* VIP Customer

Organizations may configure additional customer categories without software modifications.

---

## **Customer Profile**

Each customer profile shall maintain:

* Customer ID  
* Customer Name  
* Organization Name  
* Customer Type  
* Industry  
* Company Size  
* Country  
* Time Zone  
* Preferred Language  
* Contact Information  
* Subscription Plan  
* Products Purchased  
* Revenue Contribution  
* Customer Success Manager  
* Support Level  
* Health Score  
* Satisfaction Score  
* Churn Risk Score  
* Expansion Score  
* AI Customer Intelligence Score

---

## **Customer 360° Workspace**

The unified customer workspace shall include:

* Profile Overview  
* Contacts  
* Products & Services  
* Subscriptions  
* Success Plans  
* Onboarding Progress  
* Product Adoption  
* Learning Progress  
* Community Participation  
* Support Tickets  
* Revenue History  
* Renewal Timeline  
* Expansion Opportunities  
* Executive Notes  
* AI Recommendations  
* Audit History

# **12\. Customer Registration & Profile Management**

## **Purpose**

The Customer Registration & Profile Management Platform enables secure customer registration, profile creation, identity verification, account activation, and profile lifecycle management.

The platform shall maintain a trusted, continuously updated customer identity.

---

## **Registration Channels**

The platform shall support customer registration through:

* Website  
* Mobile Application  
* Partner Portal  
* API Integration  
* Enterprise Invitation  
* Community Platform  
* Event Registration  
* Manual Administration  
* Single Sign-On (SSO)  
* Third-Party Identity Providers

---

## **Registration Information**

Each registration shall collect:

* Full Name  
* Organization Name  
* Email Address  
* Mobile Number  
* Country  
* Preferred Language  
* Time Zone  
* Business Category  
* Job Title  
* Company Size  
* Industry  
* Referral Source  
* Marketing Preferences  
* Terms Acceptance

Administrators shall configure mandatory and optional fields.

---

## **Profile Management Features**

Customers shall be able to:

* Update profile information.  
* Manage security settings.  
* Configure communication preferences.  
* Upload profile images.  
* Manage notification settings.  
* Control privacy preferences.  
* View login history.  
* Download personal data.  
* Request account deletion.

---

## **Identity Verification**

The platform shall support:

* Email Verification  
* Mobile OTP Verification  
* Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)  
* Identity Document Verification  
* Enterprise Domain Validation  
* Risk-Based Authentication

The platform shall support:

* Geographic Segmentation  
* Demographic Segmentation

* Revenue Segmentation  
* Subscription Segmentation  
* Product Usage Segmentation  
* Customer Health Segmentation  
* Lifecycle Segmentation  
* Engagement Segmentation  
* AI Predictive Segmentation  
* Strategic Account Segmentation

Segments shall be based on:

* Product Usage  
* Revenue Contribution  
* Renewal Probability  
* Expansion Potential  
* Customer Health  
* Support History  
* Learning Activity  
* Community Participation  
* Satisfaction Scores  
* Marketing Engagement

---

## **AI Segmentation Intelligence**

Artificial Intelligence shall provide:

* Churn Risk Groups

# **14\. Customer Journey Management**

## **Purpose**

The Customer Journey Management Platform enables TBT to visualize, manage, optimize, and personalize every customer interaction across the complete customer lifecycle.

The platform shall orchestrate customer experiences using automation, AI intelligence, and configurable business workflows.

---

## **Customer Journey Stages**

The platform shall support:

1. Awareness  
2. Registration  
3. Onboarding  
4. Product Activation  
5. Product Adoption  
6. Business Value Achievement  
7. Customer Success  
8. Expansion  
9. Renewal  
10. Advocacy

Journey stages shall remain configurable.

---

## **Journey Components**

Each customer journey shall include:

* Milestones  
* Goals  
* Tasks  
* Communications  
* Events  
* Product Usage  
* Surveys  
* Reviews  
* Success Metrics  
* Executive Checkpoints

---

## **Journey Automation**

The platform shall automatically:

* Send onboarding emails.  
* Trigger success tasks.  
* Schedule business reviews.  
* Recommend learning.  
* Launch engagement campaigns.  
* Escalate risks.  
* Notify stakeholders.

Automation workflows shall remain configurable.

# **17\. Customer Intelligence Dashboard**

## **Purpose**

The Customer Intelligence Dashboard provides executives and Customer Success teams with a centralized, real-time view of customer performance, engagement, health, adoption, revenue, and strategic opportunities.

The dashboard shall consolidate operational, behavioral, financial, and AI-generated intelligence into a unified executive workspace.

---

## **Executive Dashboard Components**

The dashboard shall include:

* Total Customers  
* Active Customers  
* Customer Segments  
* Customer Health Distribution  
* Product Adoption Rate  
* Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)  
* Net Promoter Score (NPS)  
* Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)  
* Churn Risk Overview  
* Expansion Opportunities  
* Renewal Pipeline  
* Success Plan Progress  
* Executive Alerts  
* AI Customer Rankings

---

## **AI Customer Intelligence**

Artificial Intelligence shall provide:

* Health Predictions  
* Churn Forecasts  
* Adoption Recommendations  
* Expansion Opportunities  
* Customer Segmentation Insights  
* Renewal Probability  
* Executive Strategic Insights  
* Customer Growth Forecasts

Every AI-generated insight shall be transparent, configurable, explainable, and fully auditable.

---

## **Executive Reporting**

The platform shall generate configurable reports including:

* Customer Health Report  
* Customer Success Report  
* Segmentation Analysis Report  
* Customer Journey Report  
* Product Adoption Report  
* Customer Engagement Report  
* Executive Business Review Report  
* Customer Intelligence Report
