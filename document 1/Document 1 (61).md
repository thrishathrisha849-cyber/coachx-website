# **2\. Purpose**

The Enterprise Sales Management Platform enables Tamil Business Tribe (TBT) to manage the complete revenue lifecycle—from lead generation and qualification to opportunity management, account growth, customer expansion, renewals, and revenue forecasting.

The platform shall integrate marketing, sales, customer success, finance, AI intelligence, and executive reporting into one unified Revenue Operating System (RevOS).

Rather than functioning as a traditional CRM, this platform shall become an enterprise-scale Sales Intelligence and Revenue Management ecosystem capable of supporting millions of leads, opportunities, customers, and transactions.

---

# **3\. Vision**

The vision is to establish a world-class Enterprise Revenue Operating System (RevOS) that enables predictable revenue growth, data-driven sales execution, AI-assisted decision making, and continuous optimization of the complete sales process.

The platform shall help TBT:

* Accelerate revenue growth.  
* Increase sales productivity.  
* Improve lead conversion.  
* Shorten sales cycles.  
* Increase deal sizes.  
* Improve customer acquisition.  
* Increase renewal revenue.  
* Improve expansion revenue.  
* Optimize sales operations.  
* Enable executive revenue visibility.

* Customer Intelligence

# **7\. Out of Scope**

The initial platform shall not:

* Replace ERP systems.  
* Replace accounting software.  
* Automatically approve commercial contracts.  
* Execute financial settlements.  
* Replace legal review.  
* Override enterprise approval workflows.  
* Replace external payment gateways.
