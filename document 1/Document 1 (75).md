# **30\. Enterprise Customer Success Portal**

## **Purpose**

The Enterprise Customer Success Portal shall serve as the centralized digital workspace where every customer can manage their complete post-sales journey within the Tamil Business Tribe (TBT) ecosystem.

The portal shall unify onboarding, product adoption, learning, support, community participation, renewals, expansion opportunities, executive engagement, analytics, and AI-powered recommendations into a single customer experience.

The Customer Success Portal shall become the primary customer-facing operating environment after product activation.

---

## **Business Objectives**

The platform shall enable customers to:

* Complete onboarding.  
* Track business outcomes.  
* Monitor product adoption.  
* Access learning resources.  
* Raise and manage support requests.  
* Participate in community discussions.  
* Review renewals.  
* Discover expansion opportunities.  
* View business intelligence.  
* Interact with AI-powered customer success services.

---

## **Portal Modules**

The Customer Success Portal shall include:

* Customer Dashboard  
* Customer Profile  
* Success Plan  
* Product Adoption Center  
* Learning Center  
* Certification Center  
* Community  
* Support Center  
* Renewal Center  
* Expansion Center  
* Executive Reviews  
* AI Copilot  
* Reports  
* Notifications  
* Settings

Each module shall support responsive web and mobile experiences.

---

## **Customer Dashboard**

The dashboard shall display:

* Customer Health Score  
* Success Plan Progress  
* Product Adoption Score  
* Active Learning Progress  
* Support Status  
* Community Activity  
* Renewal Timeline  
* Expansion Opportunities  
* Executive Announcements  
* Recent Activities  
* AI Recommendations  
* Business Value Metrics

Dashboard widgets shall be configurable based on customer role and subscription level.

---

## **Portal Security**

The portal shall support:

* Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)  
* Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)  
* Single Sign-On (SSO)  
* Session Management  
* Device Management  
* API Security  
* Audit Logging  
* Data Encryption  
* Privacy Controls  
* Security Notifications

# **37\. Enterprise Acceptance Criteria**

## **Customer Lifecycle Management**

* Every customer shall progress through configurable lifecycle stages.  
* Onboarding, adoption, renewal, and expansion workflows shall be fully auditable.  
* Customer 360° profiles shall consolidate operational, commercial, behavioral, and AI-generated insights.  
* Success Plans shall support collaborative planning between customers and Customer Success teams.

---

## **Product Adoption & Customer Health**

* Product adoption shall be continuously monitored using configurable KPIs.  
* Customer Health Scores shall update dynamically using configurable weighting models.  
* Churn Prediction models shall provide explainable risk assessments.  
* AI recommendations shall support proactive intervention before customer health deteriorates.

---

## **Customer Experience**

* The Customer Success Portal shall provide secure access across web and mobile platforms.  
* Learning, Self-Service, Community, and Support experiences shall remain fully integrated.  
* Customer interactions shall maintain complete audit history.  
* Personalized engagement shall be driven by configurable business rules and AI intelligence.

---

## **AI & Intelligence**

* AI shall generate recommendations for onboarding, adoption, customer health, renewals, expansion, and advocacy.  
* Every AI-generated recommendation shall include confidence scores and supporting rationale.  
* AI recommendations shall remain advisory unless explicitly approved through enterprise workflows.  
* AI activities shall be fully logged for governance and compliance.

---

## **Governance & Security**

* Every customer operation shall generate immutable audit logs.  
* Sensitive customer information shall be encrypted both in transit and at rest.  
* RBAC, MFA, and SSO shall be supported throughout the Customer Success platform.  
* Executive dashboards shall provide real-time visibility into customer success performance and operational health.

---

## **Performance & Scalability**

* The platform shall support millions of customers, customer interactions, learning activities, community discussions, renewals, and AI recommendations.  
* Analytics and AI processing shall execute independently from transactional workloads.  
* The architecture shall support multi-region, multi-language, multi-currency, and multi-tenant deployments.  
* The platform shall remain extensible for future AI capabilities, customer engagement channels, enterprise integrations, and business innovations.
