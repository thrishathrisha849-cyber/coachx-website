# **1\. Purpose**

The Referral, Affiliate, Ambassador & Partner Marketing Management System shall enable Tamil Business Tribe to acquire customers, increase revenue, expand brand awareness and develop strategic distribution networks through trusted individuals, members, creators, affiliates, community leaders and business partners.

The system shall provide a unified platform for managing:

* Customer referral programs.  
* Affiliate marketing programs.  
* Brand ambassador programs.  
* Influencer partnerships.  
* Creator partnerships.  
* Reseller programs.  
* Agency partnerships.  
* Corporate partnerships.  
* Educational institution partnerships.  
* Community partnerships.  
* Strategic co-marketing agreements.

The platform shall manage the complete partner lifecycle, including recruitment, application, verification, onboarding, agreement management, tracking, attribution, commissions, rewards, payouts, communication, performance analytics, compliance and termination.

The system must support both self-service and administrator-managed programs while protecting TBT from fraud, duplicate attribution, unauthorized promotion, incorrect commissions and regulatory risk.

---

# **2\. Product Vision**

The vision is to create an enterprise-grade growth network where satisfied customers, trusted community members, creators, affiliates and organizations can promote Tamil Business Tribe products and services through transparent, measurable and mutually beneficial programs.

The platform shall transform referrals and partnerships from disconnected manual processes into a centralized, automated and scalable revenue channel.

The system shall provide:

* A unified partner database.  
* Personalized referral links and codes.  
* Accurate conversion attribution.  
* Flexible commission models.  
* Automated reward and payout processing.  
* Partner-specific campaigns.  
* Co-branded marketing assets.  
* Performance dashboards.

* Tax and compliance management.  
* AI-based partner recommendations.  
* Enterprise partner hierarchy support.  
* Multi-program participation.  
* Revenue and profitability measurement.

# **5\. Program Categories**

The platform shall support multiple program categories within one organization.

## **5.1 Customer Referral Program**

Existing customers or members invite other users and receive rewards after qualifying actions.

Examples:

* Invite a friend.  
* Refer a business owner.  
* Refer a course student.  
* Refer a premium member.  
* Refer an event attendee.  
* Refer a corporate customer.

## **5.2 Affiliate Program**

Approved affiliates promote TBT using links, codes, content or advertising and receive commissions for eligible conversions.

## **5.3 Ambassador Program**

Selected community members, experts or creators represent the TBT brand and receive rewards, benefits or commissions based on participation and performance.

## **5.4 Influencer and Creator Program**

Social media creators promote campaigns using tracked links, codes and approved creative assets.

## **5.5 Partner Program**

Organizations collaborate with TBT through lead sharing, co-marketing, product distribution, service delivery or joint business development.

## **5.6 Reseller Program**

Approved resellers sell TBT products, memberships or services directly to customers.

## **5.7 Agency Program**

Marketing, consulting or training agencies refer or manage customers for TBT and receive commissions or service fees.

## **5.8 Educational Partner Program**

Colleges, training centers, schools and educational institutions promote or distribute TBT learning programs.

## **5.9 Community Partner Program**

Professional communities, associations, clubs and networks collaborate with TBT to promote events, memberships and learning content.

# **30\. Commission Models**

The platform shall support:

## **30.1 Fixed Commission**

A fixed monetary amount is paid for each qualifying conversion.

## **30.2 Percentage Commission**

A percentage of eligible transaction value is paid.

## **30.3 Tiered Commission**

Commission rate increases when performance thresholds are reached.

## **30.4 Product-Based Commission**

Different products or plans have different commission rates.

## **30.5 Customer-Type Commission**

Commission varies for new customers, returning customers or enterprise customers.

## **30.6 Recurring Commission**

Partners receive commission on eligible subscription renewals.

## **30.7 Hybrid Commission**

A combination of fixed and percentage commission is applied.

## **30.8 Performance Bonus**

Additional bonuses are awarded after achieving defined goals.

## **30.9 Non-Monetary Reward**

Partners receive points, credits, discounts, content access or membership benefits.
