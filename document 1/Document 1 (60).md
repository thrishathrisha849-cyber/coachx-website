# **35\. Enterprise Voice of Customer (VoC) Platform**

## **Purpose**

The Enterprise Voice of Customer (VoC) Platform enables Tamil Business Tribe (TBT) to continuously capture, consolidate, analyze, prioritize, and act upon customer opinions, expectations, experiences, and suggestions across the entire customer lifecycle.

The platform shall transform customer feedback into measurable business improvements, product innovation, customer success initiatives, and strategic decision-making.

The Voice of Customer Platform shall become the organization's central source of customer intelligence.

---

## **Business Objectives**

The platform shall enable TBT to:

* Understand customer expectations.  
* Capture customer feedback continuously.  
* Improve customer satisfaction.  
* Increase product quality.  
* Identify experience gaps.  
* Detect emerging customer needs.  
* Improve customer retention.  
* Support product innovation.  
* Enable executive decision-making.  
* Build customer trust.

---

## **VoC Data Sources**

The platform shall collect customer insights from:

* Mobile Application  
* Web Portal  
* AI Assistant  
* Community Platform  
* Customer Support  
* Email  
* WhatsApp  
* Push Notification Responses  
* In-App Feedback  
* Surveys  
* Polls  
* Course Feedback  
* Webinar Feedback  
* Event Feedback  
* Product Reviews  
* App Store Reviews  
* Social Media Mentions  
* Referral Feedback  
* Customer Interviews  
* Executive Business Reviews

Additional data sources shall be configurable.

---

## **Voice Categories**

Customer feedback shall be classified into:

* Product Feedback  
* Service Feedback  
* Support Feedback  
* Community Feedback  
* Learning Experience  
* Membership Experience  
* AI Experience  
* Technical Issues  
* Feature Requests  
* Pricing Feedback  
* User Interface Feedback  
* Business Suggestions  
* General Feedback

---

## **VoC Dashboard**

The Executive VoC Dashboard shall display:

* Total Feedback Received  
* Feedback by Category  
* Satisfaction Trends  
* Sentiment Distribution  
* Top Customer Requests  
* Feature Demand  
* Customer Pain Points  
* AI Insights  
* Executive Action Queue

# **36\. Enterprise Customer Feedback Management**

## **Purpose**

The Customer Feedback Management Platform enables structured collection, classification, routing, prioritization, resolution, and tracking of customer feedback.

Every feedback item shall remain traceable from submission through implementation or closure.

---

## **Feedback Channels**

The platform shall support:

* In-App Feedback  
* Website Forms  
* Community Discussions  
* Customer Success Managers  
* AI Assistant  
* Email  
* QR Code Forms  
* Events  
* Surveys  
* Support Tickets  
* API Integrations

---

## **Feedback Information**

Each feedback record shall include:

* Feedback ID  
* Customer Information  
* Submission Date  
* Category  
* Priority  
* Journey Stage  
* Product  
* Description  
* Attachments  
* Sentiment Score  
* Assigned Team  
* Status  
* Resolution Notes

---

## **Feedback Workflow**

The lifecycle shall include:

1. Submitted  
2. Acknowledged  
3. Reviewed  
4. Prioritized  
5. Assigned  
6. In Progress  
7. Implemented  
8. Verified  
9. Closed  
10. Archived

All transitions shall generate immutable audit logs.

---

## **Feedback Prioritization**

Feedback may be prioritized using:

* Customer Impact  
* Revenue Impact  
* Strategic Alignment  
* Customer Segment  
* Frequency  
* Severity  
* Technical Complexity  
* AI Priority Score

Priority rules shall remain configurable.

# **42\. Enterprise Acceptance Criteria**

## **Customer Experience**

* Every customer interaction shall be mapped to a lifecycle stage.  
* Customer journeys shall support configurable stages and automation.  
* Omnichannel interactions shall contribute to a unified customer profile.  
* Customer experiences shall support personalization rules.

---

## **Customer Success**

* Every active customer shall maintain a configurable health score.  
* Success plans shall support milestones, tasks, and review schedules.  
* Playbooks shall trigger automatically based on configurable business conditions.  
* Customer onboarding progress shall be measurable and auditable.

---

## **Lifecycle Management**

* Lifecycle stages shall be fully configurable.  
* Customer segmentation shall support real-time dynamic updates.  
* Persona definitions shall be version-controlled.  
* Loyalty and advocacy programs shall support configurable reward rules.

---

## **Voice of Customer**

* Feedback shall be collected from multiple configurable channels.  
* Survey workflows shall support creation, distribution, analysis, and archival.  
* Sentiment analysis shall classify structured and unstructured feedback.  
* Feedback records shall maintain end-to-end traceability.

---

## **Analytics & Intelligence**

* Executive dashboards shall aggregate operational, behavioral, financial, and CX metrics.  
* AI recommendations shall include confidence scores and supporting rationale.  
* Experience analytics shall support historical trends and drill-down analysis.  
* Reports shall support scheduling, exports, and role-based visibility.

---

## **Governance & Security**

* All customer-related changes shall maintain immutable audit logs.  
* Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) shall govern customer information access.  
* Sensitive customer data shall be encrypted both at rest and in transit.  
* Privacy controls shall comply with applicable enterprise governance policies.  
* AI-assisted recommendations shall be transparent, explainable, and reviewable.

---

## **Performance & Scalability**

* The platform shall support millions of customer records, interactions, and feedback events.  
* Dashboards shall remain responsive under enterprise-scale workloads.  
* Background analytics and AI processing shall not degrade transactional performance.  
* The architecture shall support future expansion into additional customer engagement channels and enterprise business capabilities.
