# **1\. Purpose**

The Marketing Data Platform (MDP) shall serve as the **single source of truth** for every marketing, customer, community, learning, commerce, engagement and operational data generated across the Tamil Business Tribe ecosystem.

Instead of keeping customer information scattered across different systems, the platform shall unify all customer interactions into one intelligent customer profile.

The system shall continuously collect, clean, validate, enrich, classify, analyze and activate customer data for every marketing and business process.

---

# **2\. Vision**

The platform shall become an enterprise-grade Customer Intelligence Platform capable of understanding every customer interaction in real time.

It shall power:

* Marketing Automation  
* Personalization  
* Recommendation Engine  
* AI Decision Engine  
* Community Intelligence  
* Customer Journey  
* Sales Intelligence  
* Customer Success  
* Executive Analytics

through one centralized data ecosystem.

---

# **3\. Objectives**

The system shall:

* Create a unified customer profile.  
* Eliminate duplicate customer records.  
* Build a centralized marketing data warehouse.  
* Enable real-time customer intelligence.  
* Improve personalization accuracy.  
* Increase campaign effectiveness.  
* Improve AI model quality.  
* Enable enterprise reporting.  
* Support predictive analytics.  
* Maintain enterprise governance.
