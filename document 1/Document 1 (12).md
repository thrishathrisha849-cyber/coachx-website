# **1\. VOLUME PURPOSE**

This volume defines the complete Customer Relationship Management, Sales, Customer Success and Customer Support ecosystem for Tamil Business Tribe.

The system must provide one centralized platform for managing the complete customer lifecycle from the first enquiry to sales conversion, onboarding, ongoing support, renewal, retention and customer advocacy.

The CRM system must connect:

* Marketing campaigns  
* Lead generation  
* Contact management  
* Company accounts  
* Sales pipelines  
* Opportunities  
* Deals  
* Tasks  
* Calls  
* Meetings  
* Emails  
* Quotations  
* Orders  
* Payments  
* Customer onboarding  
* Customer success  
* Support tickets  
* Live chat  
* Knowledge base  
* Customer feedback  
* Automation  
* Reports  
* Administration

The module must operate as a standalone CRM and also integrate with every major TBT platform module.
