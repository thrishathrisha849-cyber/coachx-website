# **19\. Enterprise Customer Success Platform**

## **Purpose**

The Enterprise Customer Success Platform enables Tamil Business Tribe (TBT) to proactively help every customer achieve measurable business outcomes while using TBT products, memberships, AI services, learning platforms, and community ecosystems.

Unlike traditional customer support, which reacts to problems, the Customer Success Platform continuously monitors customer progress, predicts risks, recommends actions, and guides customers toward long-term success.

The platform shall become the operational hub for customer retention, expansion, and advocacy.

---

## **Business Objectives**

The platform shall enable TBT to:

* Improve customer onboarding.  
* Increase product adoption.  
* Accelerate customer activation.  
* Reduce customer churn.  
* Increase renewal rates.  
* Improve customer engagement.  
* Increase customer lifetime value (CLV).  
* Improve feature utilization.  
* Identify at-risk customers early.  
* Automate customer success workflows.  
* Support enterprise account management.  
* Build long-term customer relationships.

---

## **Core Components**

The Customer Success Platform shall include:

* Customer Health Management  
* Success Plans  
* Customer Success Tasks  
* Customer Milestones  
* Customer Goals  
* Success Playbooks  
* Customer Risk Management  
* Renewal Management  
* Expansion Opportunity Tracking  
* Customer Advocacy Management  
* AI Success Assistant  
* Executive Customer Success Analytics

---

## **Customer Success Lifecycle**

Every customer shall progress through a configurable lifecycle:

1. Registered  
2. Purchased  
3. Onboarding  
4. Activated  
5. Product Adoption  
6. Engaged  
7. Growing  
8. Renewing  
9. Expanding  
10. Advocacy  
11. Alumni  
12. Re-engagement

Each stage shall have configurable KPIs, automation rules, milestones, and AI recommendations.

---

# **20\. Enterprise Customer Health Scoring**

## **Purpose**

The Customer Health Scoring Platform continuously evaluates the health of every customer relationship using operational, behavioral, financial, and engagement data.

The score shall help Customer Success Managers prioritize proactive interventions.

---

## **Health Score Categories**

The health score shall include:

* Product Usage  
* Login Frequency  
* Feature Adoption  
* Learning Progress  
* Community Participation  
* Support History  
* Payment Status  
* Subscription Status  
* Customer Satisfaction  
* NPS  
* Renewal Probability  
* AI Engagement  
* Goal Achievement  
* Account Activity

Administrators shall configure weighting for each category.

---

## **Health Score Levels**

Customers shall be classified as:

* Excellent  
* Healthy  
* Stable  
* Attention Required  
* At Risk  
* Critical

Thresholds shall be configurable.

---

## **AI Health Intelligence**

Artificial Intelligence shall identify:

* Declining Engagement  
* Churn Signals  
* Renewal Risk  
* Upsell Opportunities  
* Customer Frustration  
* Product Misalignment  
* Success Probability  
* Recommended Interventions

Every prediction shall include confidence scores and supporting evidence.

---

## **Health Monitoring Dashboard**

The dashboard shall display:

* Overall Health Distribution  
* High-Risk Customers  
* Health Trend Analysis  
* Renewal Forecast  
* Customer Segmentation  
* AI Risk Alerts  
* Executive Summary

* Product Training
