# **1\. Purpose**

The A/B Testing, Experimentation & Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) System enables organizations using Tamil Business Tribe (TBT) to scientifically improve marketing performance through controlled experiments. Instead of relying on assumptions, the platform measures customer behavior, compares multiple experiences, and automatically identifies the highest-performing variation.

The system supports enterprise-scale experimentation across websites, landing pages, emails, push notifications, SMS, WhatsApp campaigns, pricing strategies, AI-generated content, and customer journeys.

---

# **2\. Objectives**

The CRO platform shall:

* Improve conversion rates.  
* Reduce customer acquisition cost.  
* Increase customer engagement.  
* Optimize marketing assets.  
* Validate business hypotheses.  
* Automate experiment management.  
* Deliver statistically reliable results.  
* Recommend winning variations using AI.  
* Continuously improve user experience.  
* Increase marketing ROI.
