## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Affiliate Applies, Gets Verified, and Is Approved into a Program (Priority: P1)

A content creator applies to become a TBT affiliate through the public application landing page. They submit identity, business, and promotional-reach information, accept the program terms, and pass through the review workflow (initial review, document request, verification, approval scoring) before an administrator approves them with an assigned partner tier and commission plan, and they complete a structured onboarding journey (agreement signing, tax submission, payment setup, training, link generation) before their first referral link goes live.

**Why this priority**: Without a working recruitment-to-approval-to-onboarding pipeline, no partner program of any of the nine categories can exist. This is the foundational gate every other capability (tracking, commissions, payouts) sits behind, and it is the first thing a real partner experiences.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by submitting a partner application through the public form, moving it through the review workflow to "Approved," verifying the applicant receives a structured onboarding checklist, and confirming they cannot generate a live referral link until onboarding tasks (agreement signature, tax info, payment method) are complete.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a visitor on the partner application landing page, **When** they submit the configurable application form (identity, business info, audience size, promotion methods, tax info, agreement acceptance), **Then** the system creates a Partner Application in "Submitted" status, runs duplicate-account detection, and routes it into the review workflow.
2. **Given** an application in "Under initial review," **When** a reviewer requests additional documents, **Then** the application moves to "Documents requested," the applicant is notified, and the application does not proceed to approval until the requested documents are received.
3. **Given** a reviewer approves an application with conditions, sets a partner tier, and assigns a commission plan and partner manager, **When** the decision is recorded, **Then** the applicant's status becomes "Approved," an onboarding journey is generated, and the decision plus reviewer identity are captured in the audit log.
4. **Given** an approved partner has not yet completed agreement signing, tax submission, and payment-method setup, **When** they attempt to generate a referral link, **Then** the system blocks link generation and shows the outstanding onboarding tasks and completion percentage.

### User Story 2 - Partner Earns Commission Through the Calculation Engine Under a Configured Commission Model (Priority: P1)

A referred customer completes a qualifying purchase attributed to an approved affiliate's tracking link. The Commission Calculation Engine identifies the eligible program and attributed partner, retrieves the applicable commission rule (percentage, fixed, tiered, product-based, recurring, or hybrid), applies exclusions, tier multipliers, and campaign bonuses, applies the commission cap and any tax withholding, and produces a commission record with a transparent breakdown that the partner can view on their dashboard.

**Why this priority**: Commission calculation is the economic core of the entire chapter — every program category (affiliate, ambassador, reseller, agency) exists to convert tracked activity into a correctly computed, auditable payment obligation. Without accurate calculation, every downstream capability (wallet, payout, dispute) is untrustworthy.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by configuring a commission rule (e.g., 10% of net sale, tiered by monthly revenue), driving a tracked conversion through validation to "Approved," and verifying the resulting commission record's breakdown (base amount, tier multiplier, campaign bonus, cap applied, tax withheld) matches the configured rule exactly, with the approved value reflected in the partner's wallet pending balance.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an approved conversion attributed to a partner under a percentage commission rule with a tier multiplier active, **When** the Commission Calculation Engine runs, **Then** it produces a commission record showing base commission, tier multiplier applied, campaign bonus (if any), commission cap enforcement, tax withholding (if applicable), and the final approved value, with status set to "Estimated" or "Pending validation" per the program's locking-period configuration.
2. **Given** multiple commission rules match the same conversion (e.g., a product-specific rule and a tier-based rule), **When** the engine evaluates applicable rules, **Then** the configurable priority engine selects exactly one applicable rule and records which rule was applied.
3. **Given** a program configured for recurring commission, **When** an attributed customer's subscription renews within the configured recurring duration, **Then** a new commission record is generated for the renewal event, linked to the original attribution.
4. **Given** a commission has been calculated and moved to "Approved," **When** the partner views their dashboard, **Then** they see the full breakdown (not just the final number) for that commission.

### User Story 3 - Partner Wallet Funds Move Through Pending, Locked, Approved, and Payable States to Payout (Priority: P1)

An approved partner's earned commissions accumulate in their Partner Wallet as an append-only ledger of transactions (commission credit, bonus, reversal, tax withholding, processing fee, payout). Funds move from pending to locked (during the commission locking period) to approved to payable once the locking period, refund window, and fraud review clear, and are released only through a payout that follows the configured schedule, minimum threshold, compliance check, and finance-approval workflow. Every wallet balance is a derived sum over ledger entries, never a directly editable number.

**Why this priority**: Per the Constitution's Article V (Ledger-Based Internal Economies), partner commissions must be an auditable ledger, not a mutable balance — this is a non-negotiable architectural constraint and the mechanism through which every partner is ultimately paid. Getting this wrong creates direct financial and trust risk.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by crediting a commission to a partner wallet, advancing it through the configured locking period into "Payable," triggering a scheduled payout that passes compliance and finance review, and confirming the wallet's derived balance (sum of ledger entries) matches the payout amount exactly, with every state transition individually visible in the ledger.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a newly approved commission, **When** it is credited to the partner wallet, **Then** a wallet transaction record (commission credit) is created and the wallet's pending balance is a derived sum that includes this entry — no balance field is directly overwritten.
2. **Given** a program with a 30-day commission locking period, **When** the locking period elapses without a refund, fraud flag, or chargeback, **Then** the commission moves from locked to payable and becomes eligible for the next scheduled payout run.
3. **Given** a payout batch is created for eligible payable balances, **When** it progresses through compliance check, finance review, and processing, **Then** each partner's wallet reflects a payout transaction and the payable balance is reduced by exactly the paid amount, with the full transaction history remaining visible and immutable.
4. **Given** a payout fails (e.g., invalid bank details), **When** the failure is recorded, **Then** the wallet balance is not incorrectly reduced, the partner is notified with a failure reason and a payment-method-update request, and a retry or reversal is tracked as a distinct ledger entry.

### User Story 4 - Self-Referral and Fraud Detection Blocks a Suspicious Payout Before It Is Paid (Priority: P1)

The Fraud Prevention Engine continuously scores partners, clicks, leads, and conversions for suspicious patterns (self-referral, duplicate accounts, bot clicks, cookie stuffing, coupon leakage, collusion). When a conversion's fraud risk score crosses a defined threshold — for example, matching identity signals (email, device, payment instrument, bank account) between the referring partner and the referred customer — the associated commission is placed on hold, routed to manual review, and withheld from payout until a fraud analyst resolves the case, rather than being silently paid.

**Why this priority**: Fraud prevention directly protects TBT's commission budget and program integrity; without it, every commission model and payout mechanism above is exploitable at will. It is explicitly called out as a required safeguard across self-referral detection, coupon leakage, and multi-level abuse in the source chapter.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by simulating a conversion where the referring partner's email, device, and payment instrument match the referred customer's, confirming the system computes an elevated fraud risk score, places the resulting commission "On hold," prevents it from reaching "Payable," and creates a fraud case visible to a Fraud Analyst role — while a legitimate same-household exception configured by an administrator is allowed to proceed.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a conversion where the partner identity and customer identity share matching email, device, and payment-instrument signals, **When** the conversion is evaluated, **Then** the system flags it via self-referral detection, computes a fraud risk score in the "High" or "Critical" band, and holds the associated commission rather than auto-approving it.
2. **Given** a fraud risk score in the "Critical" band (81–100), **When** the score is computed, **Then** the system may trigger commission hold, additional verification, link suspension, or account termination, and creates a fraud case record for review — but does not permanently reject the commission without human review.
3. **Given** an administrator has configured a same-household exception workflow, **When** a flagged self-referral matches the exception criteria, **Then** the transaction is routed through the exception process instead of being automatically blocked.
4. **Given** a partner-specific coupon code is detected on an unauthorized third-party coupon site, **When** coupon leakage detection identifies it, **Then** administrators can disable or replace the code, exclude resulting unauthorized conversions from commission, and warn the partner, with the action recorded in the audit log.

### User Story 5 - Multi-Level Commission Cascades with Anti-Pyramid Safeguards (Priority: P2)

In a program configured for controlled multi-level commissions, a direct affiliate's qualifying conversion generates a primary commission, and a recruiting partner and regional manager above them in the hierarchy receive secondary override and management commissions respectively — up to a configured maximum hierarchy depth. Every override commission is backed by actual revenue from the underlying conversion, never generated purely from recruitment, and the platform enforces program-specific eligibility and regulatory restrictions on the structure.

**Why this priority**: Multi-level commission is explicitly flagged in the source as legally sensitive ("where legally and commercially permitted") and carries a distinct compliance and financial risk (multi-level program abuse is a named risk in the chapter's risk table). It is lower priority than single-level commission (P1) because it is an optional, program-specific configuration rather than a universal capability.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by configuring a 3-level hierarchy (affiliate → recruiting partner → regional manager) with a maximum depth limit, driving one qualifying conversion through the direct affiliate, and confirming exactly three revenue-backed commission records are generated (one per level, each traceable to the same underlying conversion) with no commission generated for a level beyond the configured maximum depth or for recruitment alone.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a 3-level partner hierarchy with multi-level commission enabled, **When** a direct affiliate's referral converts, **Then** the direct affiliate receives the primary commission, the recruiting partner receives a secondary override, and the regional manager receives a management commission, each transparently calculated and traceable to the originating conversion.
2. **Given** a hierarchy configured with a maximum depth of 3 levels, **When** a partner exists at level 4, **Then** no override commission is generated for that level.
3. **Given** a program has not enabled commission for recruitment alone, **When** a partner recruits a new sub-partner who generates no revenue, **Then** no commission is paid for the recruitment event itself.
4. **Given** a multi-level commission cascade is calculated for a single conversion, **When** any one level's commission is reversed due to a refund, **Then** the reversal propagates correctly to the associated override and management commissions rather than leaving orphaned payable amounts.

### User Story 6 - Territory-Based Partner Hierarchy Management with Conflict Detection (Priority: P2)

An enterprise partner organization operates under a Global → National → Regional → Local → Individual Representative hierarchy, with each level assigned a defined territory (by country, state, district, city, or named account). The system detects territory conflicts (overlapping assignments), duplicate lead ownership, and unauthorized promotion outside an assigned territory, and supports parent-child roll-up reporting and override commissions across the hierarchy.

**Why this priority**: Territory management is required specifically for the enterprise partner-hierarchy scenarios (resellers, agencies, institutional/corporate partners) described in the chapter and directly prevents channel conflict, which is a real operational risk once TBT scales partner programs regionally — but it applies to a narrower set of program types than core commission/payout flows, hence P2.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by assigning two partners overlapping territory definitions (e.g., both assigned "Tamil Nadu, Chennai"), confirming the system surfaces a territory conflict alert to an administrator, and verifying that a lead generated within the disputed territory is not simultaneously attributed to both partners without resolution.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a Regional Partner assigned to a state-level territory and a Local Partner assigned to a city within that state, **When** a lead is generated in that city, **Then** the system applies the configured territory-based assignment rule and does not create duplicate lead ownership between the two partners.
2. **Given** two partners are independently assigned overlapping territory definitions, **When** the conflict is detected, **Then** the system surfaces a territory conflict alert to an administrator rather than silently allowing both to claim attribution.
3. **Given** a partner is inactive within their assigned territory for a defined period, **When** territory coverage is reviewed, **Then** the system flags territory inactivity and coverage gaps for administrator action.
4. **Given** a parent-child hierarchy (Global → National → Regional → Local), **When** an administrator requests roll-up reporting, **Then** performance and revenue figures aggregate correctly from child territories up through each parent level without double-counting.

### User Story 7 - Partner Manager Uses the Partner Portal, Disputes, and Support Workflow (Priority: P3)

An active partner logs into the responsive Partner Portal to view their dashboard (clicks, leads, conversions, commissions, wallet, next payout date), download marketing assets permitted for their tier, and — when they believe a conversion was incorrectly rejected or a commission was wrongly reversed — file a dispute with supporting evidence that is tracked through a structured review and resolution workflow.

**Why this priority**: This is the partner-facing operational experience that sustains trust and retention once the core recruitment/commission/payout/fraud mechanics (P1) and hierarchy/multi-level mechanics (P2) are in place; it is essential for day-to-day partner satisfaction but not required for the program's core financial correctness, hence P3.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by logging in as an active partner, confirming dashboard metrics match underlying tracking/commission data, downloading a tier-permitted marketing asset, and submitting a dispute against a rejected conversion that is then tracked from "Submitted" through to a recorded resolution and adjustment amount.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an active partner with clicks, leads, and conversions recorded, **When** they open the Partner Portal dashboard, **Then** displayed metrics (total clicks, conversion rate, pending/approved/paid commission, wallet balance, next payout date) match the underlying tracked and calculated records.
2. **Given** a partner's tier does not permit access to a specific marketing asset, **When** they attempt to view or download it, **Then** the system prevents access to that unauthorized asset.
3. **Given** a partner disputes a rejected conversion, **When** they submit the dispute with supporting evidence, **Then** the dispute is assigned a reviewer, tracked through defined statuses (Submitted, Under review, Information requested, Approved, Partially approved, Rejected, Escalated, Closed), and recorded with a resolution and adjustment amount where applicable.
4. **Given** a partner submits a support ticket categorized as "Payout issue," **When** the ticket is created, **Then** it is routed with the correct category and remains linked to the relevant wallet/payout record for the assigned support agent or partner manager.

### Edge Cases

- What happens when a partner-specific coupon code leaks onto an unauthorized third-party coupon site and drives a surge of conversions before detection — are those conversions retroactively excluded from commission, and how is the partner notified?
- What happens when a legitimate partner is incorrectly flagged with a high fraud risk score (false positive) and their commissions are held — what appeal or review path restores their payout without indefinite delay?
- What happens when a multi-level commission cascade is mid-calculation and one intermediate level's partner account is suspended — does the cascade still pay unaffected levels, or does the entire chain halt?
- What happens when two partners are assigned overlapping territories and each independently drives the same lead to conversion — how is the commission split or the conflict resolved rather than double-paying?
- What happens when a partner's promotional content (social post, landing page, ad) is found to violate promotional compliance rules (misleading claims, unauthorized trademark bidding) after commissions on resulting conversions have already been marked payable?
- What happens when a conversion is refunded after its associated commission has already been paid out in a completed payout batch — does the system create a negative wallet adjustment and offset it against future commissions, and what happens if the partner has no future commissions to offset against?
- What happens when a partner qualifies for a tier upgrade and a tier downgrade signal (e.g., high revenue but also a high refund rate) in the same evaluation period — which takes precedence?
- What happens when a partner operates in multiple programs simultaneously and a single customer conversion could be attributed to more than one of that partner's programs — how is cross-program attribution conflict resolved?
- What happens when a payout batch partially fails (some partner payments succeed, others fail due to invalid bank details) — are successful payments still marked paid while failures are isolated for retry?
- What happens when an application is approved but the applicant never completes mandatory onboarding training required for program activation — does the partner remain indefinitely in "Onboarding" status, and is there an expiry or escalation?

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements — Program & Partner Type Management

- **FR-001**: System MUST support at least nine distinct partner program categories: Customer Referral, Affiliate, Ambassador, Influencer/Creator, Business Partner, Reseller, Agency, Educational Institution Partner, and Community Partner programs, operable independently or sharing common partner records.
- **FR-002**: System MUST allow authorized administrators to create multiple concurrent referral and partner programs, each configured with program ID, name, category, description, owner, eligible participant type, target geography, supported language, start/end dates, application requirement, approval workflow, commission model, reward model, attribution model, cookie/tracking duration, qualifying conversion definition, payout schedule, minimum payout threshold, terms and conditions, active/inactive status, budget, performance target, and compliance requirements.
- **FR-003**: System MUST support the program status lifecycle: Draft, Pending approval, Scheduled, Active, Paused, Suspended, Closed to new applications, Completed, Archived, Cancelled — with status changes permission-controlled and recorded in the audit log.
- **FR-004**: System MUST classify each partner using one of the defined partner types (customer referrer, member referrer, affiliate, influencer, content creator, brand ambassador, community leader, educational institution, trainer, consultant, agency, reseller, distributor, technology partner, corporate partner, event partner, strategic alliance partner) and MUST allow a single partner to participate in multiple programs subject to eligibility and conflict rules.
- **FR-005**: System MUST maintain a Partner Profile containing identity information (partner ID, name, display name, contact details, address, language, timezone), business information (organization, business type, tax/business registration numbers, contacts), promotional information (social profiles, audience size/category, content categories, promotion methods, reach), and program information (programs joined, approval status, tier, assigned manager, agreement status, onboarding status, compliance status, payment status, performance score, fraud risk score).

### Functional Requirements — Partner Recruitment, Application & Verification

- **FR-006**: System MUST support public recruitment via a partner application landing page containing program information, benefits, commission overview, eligibility rules, FAQ, application form, and terms acceptance.
- **FR-007**: System MUST support invitation-based recruitment allowing administrators to invite high-value customers, community leaders, top contributors, course instructors, influencers, agencies, institutions, and existing business contacts.
- **FR-008**: System MUST support AI-assisted identification of potential partners based on referral history, community engagement, audience reach, content quality, customer satisfaction, brand alignment, purchase history, geographic influence, professional network, and historical conversion performance, with such AI-generated candidate lists treated as advisory only.
- **FR-009**: System MUST provide a configurable partner application form supporting conditional fields, file uploads, save-and-continue/draft applications, validation, duplicate detection, bot/CAPTCHA protection, and mobile responsiveness.
- **FR-010**: System MUST route applications through a review workflow with statuses: Draft, Submitted, Under initial review, Documents requested, Verification in progress, Interview required, Compliance review, Approved, Conditionally approved, Rejected, Waitlisted, Withdrawn.
- **FR-011**: System MUST allow reviewers to add internal notes, request information, reassign a reviewer, schedule an interview, approve with conditions, reject with a documented reason, set partner tier, select a commission plan, and assign a partner manager.
- **FR-012**: System MUST support partner verification steps including email, phone, identity, address, bank account, tax profile, business registration, website ownership, and social account verification, plus sanctions/compliance screening and duplicate account detection, with verification status visible to authorized administrators and the partner.
- **FR-013**: System MUST support an approval scoring calculation using audience relevance, audience quality, brand alignment, content quality, promotional experience, geographic relevance, engagement rate, past performance, reputation signals, compliance risk, fraud risk, and expected revenue potential, and MUST NOT allow AI recommendations to automatically approve high-risk applicants without human review (consistent with the AI-assistive-never-autonomous principle).

### Functional Requirements — Partner Onboarding, Training & Agreements

- **FR-014**: System MUST provide a structured onboarding journey for approved partners covering welcome communication, account activation, profile completion, identity verification, agreement signing, tax information submission, payment method setup, training completion, brand guideline review, promotional policy acceptance, campaign selection, referral link generation, asset download, test referral validation, and partner activation, with the onboarding dashboard showing completion percentage and outstanding tasks.
- **FR-015**: System MUST provide partner training resources (program overview, product knowledge, membership benefits, brand positioning, sales scripts, content guidelines, advertising policies, disclosure requirements, referral-link usage, commission rules, fraud-prevention policies, support process) in video, course, ebook, webinar, quiz, certification, and live orientation formats, and MUST support requiring mandatory training completion before program activation.
- **FR-016**: System MUST support digital agreements for affiliates, ambassadors, and partners containing agreement ID/type, program, partner, effective/expiration dates, renewal rules, commission terms, payment terms, promotion restrictions, confidentiality terms, intellectual property terms, data protection obligations, termination clauses, jurisdiction, and signatory/signature status.
- **FR-017**: System MUST support agreement templates, version control, electronic signatures, renewal reminders, amendment workflows, agreement suspension, agreement termination, and archived copies.

### Functional Requirements — Links, Codes, Tracking & Attribution

- **FR-018**: System MUST generate unique referral links containing partner ID, program ID, campaign ID, product ID, landing page ID, source/medium identifiers, custom tracking parameters, and expiration date, and MUST let partners generate, copy, shorten, create QR codes for, label by campaign, view performance of, disable, and generate mobile deep links for approved links.
- **FR-019**: System MUST generate unique referral and promotional codes (personal referral, campaign, discount, event, membership, product, partner-specific coupon, offline tracking) with administrator-configurable format, validity period, usage limit, customer/product eligibility, discount value, commission eligibility, geographic/channel restrictions, and stackability rules.
- **FR-020**: System MUST support QR code generation linked to referral links, product pages, membership checkout, event registration, course enrollment, ebook download, app installation, or custom landing pages, and MUST capture scan count, unique scans, device type, approximate location (where legally permitted), landing page visits, leads, conversions, and revenue.
- **FR-021**: System MUST support deep linking into the mobile application, specific courses, ebooks, podcast episodes, event registration, community pages, membership plans, product checkout, and referral registration screens, and when the app is not installed MUST redirect to the correct app store or web fallback while preserving attribution where technically possible.
- **FR-022**: System MUST allow administrators to configure referral attribution duration per program (options from same-session up to lifetime attribution, including custom durations), with separate configurable durations for click-through, referral-code, lead, purchase, renewal, and view-through attribution.
- **FR-023**: System MUST support configurable attribution models (first referral source, last referral source, last non-direct referral, partner-priority, referral-code priority, link-click priority, lead-owner, multi-touch, shared-partner, custom) and MUST display the active attribution model in program settings and on every commission record.
- **FR-024**: System MUST record click data (click ID, partner ID, link ID, program ID, campaign ID, timestamp, region, device, browser, OS, referring/landing page, session ID, customer ID where available, bot classification, fraud status, consent status) and MUST exclude invalid and bot-generated clicks from payable performance metrics.
- **FR-025**: System MUST maintain a referred-lead record (lead ID, referring partner, referral link/code, source, creation date, status, qualification status, lead score, assigned sales representative, product interest, estimated value, conversion status, disqualification reason, fraud status), and MUST restrict partner visibility of lead information to what the program's privacy configuration permits.
- **FR-026**: System MUST support tracking of defined conversion event types (registration, verified registration, lead creation, qualified lead, consultation booking, webinar registration, event attendance, course purchase, ebook purchase, membership purchase, subscription activation/renewal, membership upgrade, referral success, custom revenue event), each recorded with conversion ID, partner ID, customer ID, program ID, campaign ID, transaction ID, product, conversion amount, eligible commission amount, currency, validation status, refund status, and commission status.
- **FR-027**: System MUST validate every conversion before commission approval against successful payment, minimum purchase amount, customer eligibility, new-customer requirement, self-referral detection, duplicate order detection, refund waiting period, chargeback status, coupon eligibility, geographic eligibility, program eligibility, fraud score, attribution window, and product eligibility, tracked through statuses: Pending, Under validation, Approved, Rejected, Reversed, On hold, Disputed, Paid.

### Functional Requirements — Commission Engine

- **FR-028**: System MUST support the following commission models: fixed commission, percentage commission, tiered commission, product-based commission, customer-type commission, recurring commission, hybrid (fixed + percentage) commission, performance bonus, and non-monetary reward (points, credits, discounts, content access, membership benefits).
- **FR-029**: System MUST support configurable commission rules scoped by program, partner type, partner tier, product, membership plan, customer segment, geographic region, campaign, conversion type, revenue range, new/existing customer, first/repeat purchase, commission percentage, fixed commission, maximum commission, minimum transaction value, recurring duration, effective/expiration dates, and approval requirement, and MUST resolve multiple matching rules through a configurable priority engine.
- **FR-030**: System MUST support a tiered commission structure (e.g., Starter, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Elite) where tier calculation may weigh valid clicks, qualified leads, approved conversions, revenue, customer quality, refund rate, compliance score, partner tenure, training completion, and campaign participation.
- **FR-031**: System MUST support controlled multi-level commission structures where legally and commercially permitted, enforcing a maximum hierarchy depth, program-specific eligibility, transparent payout calculations, regulatory restrictions, anti-pyramid safeguards, revenue-backed commissions only, and MUST NOT pay commission solely for recruitment unless legally approved for that program.
- **FR-032**: System MUST support revenue-sharing configuration for strategic partners including gross/net revenue basis, eligible products/customers, revenue-share percentage, refund treatment, tax treatment, payment schedule, maximum amount, minimum guarantee, contract duration, and renewal terms.
- **FR-033**: System MUST support customer-referral rewards distinct from partner commissions (TBT points, wallet credits, cashback, coupons, membership discounts, free membership duration, course/ebook access, event tickets, badges, community recognition, physical rewards, custom benefits) including two-sided rewards for both referrer and referred customer.
- **FR-034**: System MUST require reward qualification conditions (verified registration, first successful payment, minimum order value, refund-period completion, membership activation, course completion, event attendance, program eligibility, unique-customer requirement, fraud validation) before issuing a referral reward, and MUST NOT issue rewards for cancelled, refunded, duplicated, or fraudulent conversions.
- **FR-035**: System MUST run the Commission Calculation Engine to: receive an approved conversion, identify the eligible program, identify the attributed partner, retrieve applicable commission rules, determine eligible revenue, apply exclusions, calculate base commission, apply tier multiplier, apply campaign bonus, apply commission cap, apply tax withholding where required, create the commission record, route it for approval or auto-approve, and add the approved value to the partner wallet — with every calculation including a transparent breakdown.
- **FR-036**: System MUST track commission status through: Estimated, Pending validation, Approved, Rejected, Locked, Payable, Paid, Reversed, Disputed, Expired, On hold — and MUST let partners view the reason for any rejected or reversed commission, subject to privacy and fraud-investigation restrictions.
- **FR-037**: System MUST, on refund/cancellation/chargeback of a transaction, identify the associated commission, mark it under review, reverse unpaid commission, create a negative wallet adjustment when necessary, offset future commissions where permitted, notify the partner, record the reason, preserve original transaction history, update program analytics, and apply proportionate commission adjustments for partial refunds when configured.
- **FR-038**: System MUST support a program-configurable commission locking period (immediate up to a custom duration) during which payment confirmation, refund-window completion, fraud review, subscription activation, product-delivery verification, and customer-eligibility verification occur before a commission becomes payable.

### Functional Requirements — Partner Wallet & Payout

- **FR-039**: System MUST maintain a Partner Wallet per eligible partner tracking pending balance, approved balance, locked balance, payable balance, paid total, reversed amount, tax withheld, lifetime earnings, and current currency, where every balance is a derived sum over an append-only, auditable, immutable ledger of wallet transactions (commission credit, referral reward, bonus, manual adjustment, reversal, payout, tax withholding, processing fee, currency conversion) — never a directly writable balance field, per the Ledger-Based Internal Economies principle.
- **FR-040**: System MUST support payout methods including bank transfer, UPI, digital wallet, payment provider transfer, TBT wallet credit, gift card, store credit, manual corporate invoice, and international transfer, with availability varying by country, partner type, and compliance requirements.
- **FR-041**: System MUST support configurable payout schedules (on demand, weekly, biweekly, monthly, quarterly, custom contractual) with rules for minimum payout threshold, maximum payout limit, hold period, payment method, partner currency, tax deduction, processing fee, compliance verification, and finance approval.
- **FR-042**: System MUST run payouts through the workflow: Eligible, Requested or scheduled, Compliance check, Finance review, Approved, Processing, Paid, Failed, Reversed, Reconciled — and MUST record failure reason, offer a retry option, request a payment-method update, notify the partner, and provide a finance action path for failed payouts.
- **FR-043**: System MUST support tax management including tax identification number, tax residency, business tax registration, withholding category and percentage, tax declaration, invoice requirement, tax certificate, and country-specific documentation, and MUST support tax withholding, payout statements, tax reports, annual earnings summaries, downloadable certificates, and finance exports, with final tax treatment following applicable jurisdictional rules and approved finance policy.
- **FR-044**: System MUST let partners, where required, upload invoices, generate invoices from approved earnings, view invoice templates, submit tax details, track invoice approval, correct rejected invoices, and download payment receipts, tracked through statuses: Draft, Submitted, Under review, Approved, Rejected, Paid, Cancelled.
- **FR-045**: System MUST support multi-currency operation (program currency, partner wallet currency, transaction currency, payout currency, organization base currency, exchange-rate source and date, currency conversion fee, historical exchange rates) with currency conversion rules displayed transparently in payout statements.
- **FR-046**: System MUST support payout reconciliation comparing approved payout records, payment provider records, bank transfer records, wallet transactions, partner invoices, tax deductions, failed payments, and reversals, tracked through statuses: Unmatched, Partially matched, Matched, Disputed, Reconciled, Closed.

### Functional Requirements — Fraud Prevention

- **FR-047**: System MUST detect suspicious activity involving self-referrals, duplicate accounts, fake registrations, bot clicks, click flooding, cookie stuffing, coupon leakage, unauthorized paid advertising, trademark bidding, duplicate payment methods, repeated device use, abnormal conversion rate, high refund rate, geographic anomalies, incentivized-traffic violations, and collusion between partners and customers.
- **FR-048**: System MUST compute a fraud risk score (0–100, banded Low/Moderate/Elevated/High/Critical) for each partner, click, lead, and conversion, and high-risk activity MUST be capable of triggering commission hold, manual review, partner notification, additional verification, link suspension, program suspension, account termination, or legal/compliance escalation — with no automatic permanent rejection of a commission absent human review of high-risk cases.
- **FR-049**: System MUST perform self-referral detection by comparing partner and customer identity signals (email, phone, device, payment instrument, IP-derived region, address, bank account, tax identifier, organization), while supporting configurable exception workflows for legitimate same-household or same-organization transactions.
- **FR-050**: System MUST detect coupon leakage by identifying partner-specific codes appearing on unauthorized coupon sites, public forums, unapproved ad campaigns, search engine ads, or third-party marketplaces, and MUST let administrators disable leaked codes, replace codes, exclude unauthorized conversions, warn partners, suspend commissions, and update program policies.
- **FR-051**: System MUST enforce promotional compliance rules covering brand/logo usage, content claims, pricing statements, discount communication, affiliate disclosure, paid advertising, email/SMS/WhatsApp marketing, trademark bidding, domain usage, social media conduct, prohibited content, misleading claims, and spam prevention, with a compliance review workflow (Submitted, Under review, Approved, Approved with changes, Rejected, Expired, Withdrawn) covering partner websites, social posts, ad screenshots, email campaigns, landing pages, videos, promotional claims, discount messages, and co-branded content.

### Functional Requirements — Marketing Assets & Partner Campaigns

- **FR-052**: System MUST provide a Marketing Asset Library (logos, brand guidelines, banner ads, social posts, product images, videos, reels, email/WhatsApp/SMS templates, landing page templates, sales presentations, brochures, case studies, testimonials, QR posters, event materials) restrictable by program, partner type, tier, geography, language, product, campaign, effective date, agreement status, and training status, and MUST prevent access to expired, withdrawn, or unauthorized assets.
- **FR-053**: System MUST version-control each marketing asset (asset ID, version, title, description, category, format, language, campaign, product, created-by, approved-by, effective/expiration date, usage restrictions, download count) and MUST notify partners when a previously downloaded asset is replaced or withdrawn.
- **FR-054**: System MUST let eligible partners personalize approved co-branded templates (partner name, logo, contact details, referral code/link, QR code, regional information, event details) within locked brand guidelines, requiring approval before publication where configured.
- **FR-055**: System MUST allow administrators to create partner-specific campaigns (objective, period, eligible partners, products, audience, assets, referral links, commission bonus, performance targets, compliance instructions, training resources, leaderboard) that partners may accept, decline, or request more information about.

### Functional Requirements — Partner Communication, Support & Disputes

- **FR-057**: System MUST provide a Partner Support System (help articles, FAQ, support tickets with evidence upload, commission disputes, payout issue reports, link support requests, asset requests, partner manager contact) with ticket categories including account, tracking, conversion, commission, payout, tax, agreement, marketing compliance, technical issue, and general inquiry.
- **FR-058**: System MUST support Partner Dispute Management for missing referrals, rejected conversions, reversed commissions, incorrect commission rates, failed payouts, attribution conflicts, and fraud classifications, with each dispute recording dispute ID, related transaction, partner explanation, supporting evidence, assigned reviewer, status, internal notes, resolution, adjustment amount, and resolution date, tracked through statuses: Submitted, Under review, Information requested, Approved, Partially approved, Rejected, Escalated, Closed.

### Functional Requirements — Partner Portal & Dashboards

- **FR-059**: System MUST provide a responsive Partner Portal (desktop, tablet, mobile) with dashboard, program information, referral links/codes/QR codes, campaigns, marketing assets, leads, conversions, commissions, wallet, payouts, invoices, tax documents, training, agreements, support, notifications, and profile settings.
- **FR-060**: System MUST provide a partner dashboard showing total/unique clicks, leads, qualified leads, conversions, conversion rate, revenue generated, pending/approved/paid/reversed commission, average order value, refund rate, partner tier, target progress, active campaigns, wallet balance, and next payout date.
- **FR-061**: System MUST provide an administrator dashboard showing total active programs, total partners, new applications, approved partners, active affiliates/ambassadors/business partners, referral clicks/leads/conversions, partner-generated revenue, total commissions, pending payouts, fraud alerts, top and underperforming partners, partner retention rate, and program ROI.
- **FR-062**: System MUST provide an executive-level dashboard showing partner-sourced and partner-influenced revenue, customer acquisition cost, commission cost, program profitability, return on partner investment, revenue by program/partner type, top-performing markets, partner growth/churn, referral customer lifetime value, and fraud loss prevented, including an AI-generated executive summary with recommended strategic actions that requires human review before any resulting action is taken.

### Functional Requirements — Partner Performance, Lifecycle & Retention

## Key Entities *(include if feature involves data)*

- **Program**: A configured referral/partner program of one of the nine categories; holds category, eligibility, commission/reward/attribution model, cookie duration, payout schedule, budget, status, and compliance requirements.
- **Partner**: A person or organization enrolled in one or more programs; holds identity/business/promotional profile data, partner type, tier, assigned manager, agreement/onboarding/compliance/payment status, performance score, and fraud risk score. May represent a customer referrer, affiliate, ambassador, influencer, reseller, agency, institution, or corporate/strategic partner.
- **Partner Application**: A submitted application record moving through review statuses (Draft through Approved/Rejected/Waitlisted), holding applicant-supplied fields, reviewer notes, verification results, and approval score.
- **Partner Organization**: A business entity behind a partner (agency, institution, reseller, corporate partner) that may have multiple users, branches, and a contact hierarchy.
- **Agreement**: A versioned digital contract binding a partner to a program, with commission terms, payment terms, restrictions, IP/data-protection/termination clauses, and signature status.
- **Referral / Referral Code / Tracking Link**: The trackable mechanisms (unique link, code, QR code, deep link) a partner uses to drive attributable traffic; each carries partner/program/campaign identifiers, tracking parameters, and expiration.
- **Campaign**: A partner-specific promotional initiative with objective, period, eligible partners, assets, commission bonus, and performance targets.
- **Marketing Asset**: A version-controlled promotional resource (logo, banner, template, video, etc.) restricted by program/tier/geography/language/date and tracked for download and replacement.
- **Click**: A tracked visit through a partner link/code, holding device/browser/region/session/bot-classification/fraud-status data; excluded from payable metrics when invalid.
- **Lead**: A referred prospect record with qualification status, score, assigned sales rep, and conversion/fraud status, visibility-restricted per program privacy configuration.
- **Conversion**: A qualifying revenue or non-revenue event attributed to a partner, holding transaction reference, amount, eligible commission amount, validation status, refund status, and commission status.
- **Commission Rule**: A configurable rule scoping which commission model, rate, cap, and conditions apply for a given program/partner-type/tier/product/segment/region/campaign combination, resolved via a priority engine when multiple rules match.
- **Commission**: A calculated payment obligation tied to one conversion, with a transparent breakdown (base, tier multiplier, bonus, cap, tax withholding) and a lifecycle status (Estimated through Paid/Reversed/Disputed).
- **Partner Wallet**: An append-only ledger of wallet transactions (credit, reward, bonus, adjustment, reversal, payout, withholding, fee, conversion) per partner; all balances (pending/locked/approved/payable/paid/reversed/lifetime) are derived sums, never directly writable fields.
- **Payout**: A scheduled or on-demand disbursement of a partner's payable wallet balance, progressing through compliance/finance-review/processing/paid/failed/reconciled states.
- **Fraud Risk Score / Fraud Case**: A computed 0–100 risk score (banded Low to Critical) attached to a partner, click, lead, or conversion, and the investigation case record opened when a threshold is crossed.
- **Partner Hierarchy Level**: A node in an enterprise partner hierarchy (Global/National/Regional/Local/Individual Representative) with parent-child relationships and override-commission eligibility.
- **Territory**: A geographic or segment-based assignment (country/state/district/city/postal code/language/industry/segment/product/named account) mapped to a partner or hierarchy level, subject to conflict and coverage-gap detection.
- **Partner Tier**: A performance/compliance-based classification (e.g., Starter through Elite) that governs commission rate, asset access, and benefits, assignable automatically, manually, or contractually.
- **Dispute**: A partner-initiated challenge to a rejected conversion, reversed commission, or failed payout, tracked from submission through resolution with an adjustment amount.
- **Audit Record**: An immutable log entry capturing actor, timestamp, action, previous/new value, and affected entity for every material system action.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: An approved partner can go from application submission to a live, working referral link in under the program's configured onboarding SLA, with 100% of onboarding tasks (agreement, tax, payment method) enforced before link activation.
- **SC-002**: Commission calculations for every supported commission model (fixed, percentage, tiered, product-based, recurring, hybrid) produce a breakdown that reconciles exactly with the configured rule in 100% of automated regression test cases.
- **SC-003**: Partner wallet balances at any point in time are always fully reconstructable as the exact derived sum of their ledger transactions, with zero instances of a directly-edited balance field in audit review.
- **SC-004**: Referral link/code generation completes in under 1 second, referral code validation in under 500 milliseconds, click tracking in under 300 milliseconds, and commission calculation in under 5 seconds, matching the source chapter's stated performance targets.
- **SC-005**: At least 95% of self-referral and coupon-leakage fraud cases are placed on hold before payout (not after), and no commission reaches "Paid" status while an open, unresolved Critical-band fraud case exists against the same conversion.
- **SC-006**: Multi-level commission cascades never exceed the program's configured maximum hierarchy depth, and 100% of override/management commissions are traceable to a specific revenue-generating conversion (zero recruitment-only payouts in programs not configured to allow them).
- **SC-007**: Territory conflict detection surfaces 100% of newly created overlapping territory assignments to an administrator before any lead is auto-assigned under the conflicting configuration.
- **SC-008**: Partner-reported dispute resolution reaches a recorded status (approved, partially approved, rejected, or escalated) within the program's defined SLA for at least 90% of submitted disputes.
- **SC-009**: Payout batches reconcile against payment provider and bank records with a "Matched" or "Reconciled" status for at least 99% of individual payout line items per batch.
- **SC-010**: Every AI Partner Manager recommendation acted upon by an administrator has an associated evidence, confidence, and risk-level record, and zero consequential partner actions (approval, suspension, tier change, commission adjustment) are shown to have been taken by AI without a human decision recorded in the audit log.

## Assumptions

- Feature 009 (membership-payments-revenue) already defines the transactional/financial source of truth for referral and affiliate mechanics that occur at the point of purchase (order-level affiliate/referral attribution fields, coupon types, referral/affiliate fraud signals feeding into checkout-time fraud scoring, the affiliate commission lifecycle tied to Order/Payment records, and the ledger accounts for affiliate/referral payable). This spec (030) assumes that transactional layer as a dependency and focuses on the broader multi-program (9 program types), multi-level, hierarchy/territory, partner-lifecycle, marketing-asset, and AI-partner-intelligence capabilities described in Volume 14 Part 1 Chapter 17 that go beyond a single membership-purchase affiliate flow. Where both specs describe overlapping mechanics (e.g., commission calculation basis, wallet ledger entries, self-referral detection), this spec treats 009's transactional definitions as authoritative for the purchase/order layer and adds the program-management, hierarchy, and cross-program orchestration layer on top.
- Feature 046 (partner-relationship-management, Volume 14 Part 2 Chapter 13) is expected to extend partner management at enterprise/PRM scale (deeper account-based partner relationship workflows, enterprise deal registration, and channel-partner CRM capabilities) beyond what this chapter defines. This spec covers the marketing-originated partner acquisition, commission, wallet, and lifecycle mechanics; feature 046 should be treated as the enterprise PRM layer that consumes and extends this spec's Partner and Partner Organization entities rather than redefining them. Any enterprise-PRM-specific requirement not present in Chapter 17's source text is out of scope here and deferred to 046.
- "Where legally and commercially permitted" (multi-level commission, per source Section 33) is read as requiring per-program, per-jurisdiction legal/compliance sign-off before multi-level commission is enabled for that program; this spec does not define the specific jurisdictions or legal thresholds where multi-level programs are permitted.
- The source chapter does not specify exact numeric fraud-score thresholds beyond the stated bands (0–20 Low through 81–100 Critical) or exact SLA durations for dispute resolution/onboarding; this spec treats those as program-configurable rather than hard-coded, consistent with the chapter's repeated use of "configurable" and "program-specific."
- Payment rails, tax-withholding calculation logic, and invoice numbering/GST-compliance mechanics themselves are assumed to be provided by the finance/payment infrastructure defined in feature 009, with this spec only defining the partner-facing tax-profile collection and payout-trigger behavior described in Sections 42–46 of the source chapter.
- Identity verification, electronic signature, and fraud-detection services are assumed to be provided by external/shared platform services (per Section 112, Dependencies) rather than built natively within this feature.
