# **1\. Purpose**

The Omnichannel Marketing Orchestration, Customer Journey Automation & Real-Time Engagement Platform shall enable Tamil Business Tribe to coordinate personalized customer interactions across every supported communication, content, commerce, community and service channel.

The system shall ensure that customers receive consistent, relevant and timely experiences regardless of where they interact with TBT.

Supported interaction environments shall include:

* Website.  
* Mobile application.  
* Email.  
* SMS.  
* WhatsApp.  
* Push notifications.  
* Browser notifications.  
* In-app messages.  
* Social media.  
* Community feeds.  
* Direct messaging.  
* Voice channels.  
* Events.  
* Webinars.  
* Courses.  
* Ebooks.  
* Podcasts.  
* Customer support.  
* Sales interactions.  
* Partner and referral channels.

The platform shall monitor customer behavior continuously and automatically determine the next most appropriate communication, offer, content item, action or human intervention.

---

# **2\. Product Vision**

The vision is to create a unified, intelligent and real-time customer engagement operating system for Tamil Business Tribe.

Instead of managing each marketing channel independently, the platform shall coordinate all channels around one customer profile, one consent framework, one journey history and one decision engine.

The platform shall provide:

* Unified customer journeys.  
* Cross-channel campaign coordination.  
* Real-time event processing.  
* Behavior-triggered engagement.  
* Personalized message selection.  
* Channel-priority management.  
* Contact-frequency control.  
* AI-based next-best-action recommendations.  
* Journey optimization.  
* Customer-level engagement history.  
* Revenue and conversion attribution.  
* Journey analytics.  
* Human and AI collaboration.  
* Enterprise governance.

# **29\. Journey Conflict Resolution**

The platform shall identify conflicting actions such as:

* Promotion and refund communication at the same time.  
* Renewal reminder after renewal completion.  
* Upgrade offer after cancellation.  
* Multiple discounts for the same product.  
* Sales outreach during an unresolved complaint.  
* Welcome message after account closure.  
* Repeated abandoned-cart reminders.  
* Contradictory membership offers.

Conflict-resolution methods include:

* Suppress lower-priority action.  
* Delay lower-priority action.  
* Replace action.  
* Merge compatible communications.  
* Escalate for manual review.  
* Exit the lower-priority journey.

# **110\. Risks and Mitigation**

| Risk | Mitigation |
| ----- | ----- |
| Customers receive too many messages | Global frequency limits and fatigue scoring |
| Conflicting campaigns | Journey priority and conflict-resolution engine |
| Messages sent without consent | Consent validation before every communication |
| Duplicate journey entry | Re-entry rules and idempotent processing |
| Channel provider outage | Provider failover and fallback channels |
| Incorrect personalization | Required fallback values and preview testing |
| Journey logic loop | Journey validation and maximum loop controls |
| Customer exits not processed | Real-time goal and exit evaluation |
| High messaging costs | Budget limits and channel-cost optimization |
| AI makes unsuitable recommendation | Human approval, evidence and risk controls |
| Customer data exposed | Field-level security, masking and tenant isolation |
| Real-time processing delays | Queue monitoring, scaling and SLA alerts |
| Sales and marketing conflict | Shared customer timeline and journey suppression |
| Journey changes affect active customers | Versioning and migration controls |
| Revenue attribution is duplicated | Integration with governed attribution engine |
