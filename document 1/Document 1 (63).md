# **19\. Enterprise Opportunity Management**

## **Purpose**

The Enterprise Opportunity Management Platform enables Tamil Business Tribe (TBT) to identify, manage, prioritize, monitor, and successfully close revenue opportunities throughout the complete enterprise sales lifecycle.

The platform shall provide a centralized workspace where sales teams, customer success teams, finance, product teams, executives, and AI intelligence collaborate to maximize revenue outcomes.

Every opportunity shall maintain complete visibility from qualification through contract execution, onboarding, expansion, and long-term customer success.

---

## **Business Objectives**

The platform shall enable TBT to:

* Increase opportunity conversion.  
* Improve sales visibility.  
* Shorten enterprise sales cycles.  
* Increase average deal size.  
* Improve revenue predictability.  
* Reduce opportunity leakage.  
* Improve collaboration.  
* Support enterprise account selling.  
* Improve executive decision-making.  
* Maximize long-term customer value.

---

## **Opportunity Categories**

The platform shall support configurable opportunity types including:

* New Business  
* Membership Sales  
* Course Sales  
* Enterprise Sales  
* Government Opportunities  
* AI Product Sales  
* SaaS Subscription  
* Annual Renewal  
* Upsell  
* Cross-Sell  
* Partnership Opportunity  
* Strategic Account Opportunity  
* Expansion Opportunity

Administrators shall be able to define additional opportunity categories without software modifications.

---

## **Opportunity Information**

Each opportunity shall include:

* Opportunity ID  
* Opportunity Name  
* Associated Account  
* Primary Contact  
* Opportunity Owner  
* Sales Team  
* Business Unit  
* Product(s)  
* Expected Revenue  
* Forecast Category  
* Expected Close Date  
* Opportunity Source  
* Sales Stage  
* Win Probability  
* Health Score  
* AI Confidence Score  
* Risk Level  
* Competitors  
* Documents  
* Activity Timeline  
* Executive Sponsor

---

## **Opportunity Workspace**

Each opportunity shall maintain a unified workspace containing:

* Customer Profile  
* Communication History  
* Meeting Notes  
* Proposal Documents  
* Contract Versions  
* Product Demonstrations  
* AI Recommendations  
* Sales Tasks  
* Internal Collaboration  
* Approval History  
* Opportunity Analytics

---

# **20\. Enterprise Opportunity Qualification**

## **Purpose**

The Opportunity Qualification Platform evaluates whether a qualified lead represents a viable, strategically aligned, and financially valuable business opportunity.

Qualification ensures that sales resources focus on opportunities with the highest probability of success.

---

## **Qualification Framework**

The platform shall support:

* MEDDICC  
* MEDDPICC  
* BANT  
* CHAMP  
* SPICED  
* SPIN  
* Custom Enterprise Frameworks

Organizations may configure proprietary qualification methodologies.

---

## **Qualification Criteria**

The platform shall evaluate:

* Business Need  
* Strategic Alignment  
* Decision Authority  
* Budget Availability  
* Purchase Timeline  
* Product Fit  
* Customer Readiness  
* Competitive Position  
* Revenue Potential  
* Renewal Opportunity  
* Expansion Potential  
* AI Intent Score

---

## **Qualification Outcomes**

Each opportunity shall be classified as:

* Highly Qualified  
* Qualified  
* Developing  
* Needs Validation  
* High Risk  
* Disqualified

Thresholds shall remain configurable.

---

## **AI Qualification Intelligence**

Artificial Intelligence shall provide:

* Opportunity Quality Score  
* Buying Intent Prediction  
* Stakeholder Influence Analysis  
* Competitive Risk Assessment  
* Revenue Probability  
* Recommended Qualification Actions

Every recommendation shall remain explainable and reviewable.

---

# **21\. Enterprise Opportunity Lifecycle**

## **Purpose**

The Opportunity Lifecycle Platform manages every opportunity through standardized, measurable, and auditable sales stages until successful completion or closure.

---

## **Opportunity Lifecycle Stages**

The platform shall support configurable lifecycle stages including:

1. Opportunity Created  
2. Discovery  
3. Qualification  
4. Solution Alignment  
5. Demonstration  
6. Proposal Preparation  
7. Proposal Submitted  
8. Negotiation  
9. Executive Review  
10. Contract Review  
11. Approval  
12. Closed Won  
13. Closed Lost  
14. Onboarding  
15. Expansion  
16. Renewal

Organizations may customize lifecycle stages according to business requirements.

---

## **Stage Management**

Each stage shall define:

* Required Activities  
* Mandatory Documents  
* Responsible Teams  
* Entry Conditions  
* Exit Criteria  
* Approval Requirements  
* Risk Indicators  
* KPIs  
* Automation Rules

No opportunity shall advance unless mandatory validation rules are satisfied.

---

## **Lifecycle Tracking**

The platform shall monitor:

* Stage Duration  
* Average Time in Stage  
* Stage Conversion Rate  
* Opportunity Velocity  
* Bottlenecks  
* Approval Delays  
* Revenue Progression  
* Historical Performance

# **22\. Enterprise Opportunity Forecasting**

## **Purpose**

The Opportunity Forecasting Platform enables revenue teams to accurately predict future revenue using pipeline data, AI forecasting models, historical trends, and opportunity intelligence.

Forecasts shall support operational planning and executive decision-making.

---

## **Forecast Categories**

Supported forecast categories include:

* Pipeline  
* Best Case  
* Most Likely  
* Commit  
* Closed  
* Upside  
* Risk

Forecast models shall be configurable.

---

## **Forecast Inputs**

The platform shall consider:

* Opportunity Stage  
* Win Probability  
* Historical Close Rates  
* Sales Representative Performance  
* Product Mix  
* Customer Segment  
* Sales Cycle Duration  
* Revenue History  
* Market Conditions  
* AI Predictions

---

## **Forecast Metrics**

The platform shall calculate:

* Forecast Revenue  
* Monthly Forecast  
* Quarterly Forecast  
* Annual Forecast  
* Revenue Variance  
* Forecast Accuracy  
* Opportunity Confidence  
* Pipeline Coverage  
* Revenue Gap

---

## **AI Revenue Forecasting**

Artificial Intelligence shall:

* Predict revenue outcomes.  
* Estimate close dates.  
* Detect forecast risks.  
* Recommend pipeline improvements.  
* Identify revenue gaps.  
* Suggest corrective actions.

All predictions shall include confidence levels and historical justification.

# **24\. Enterprise Deal Management**

## **Purpose**

The Enterprise Deal Management Platform manages the commercial aspects of every sales opportunity from proposal creation through contract execution and revenue realization.

The platform shall support structured governance, approvals, pricing controls, and executive visibility.

---

## **Deal Components**

Each deal shall include:

* Deal ID  
* Opportunity Reference  
* Customer Account  
* Products & Services  
* Pricing  
* Discounts  
* Taxes  
* Payment Terms  
* Contract Duration  
* Renewal Terms  
* Deal Value  
* Currency  
* Commercial Risks  
* Approval Status

---

## **Deal Workflow**

Every deal shall progress through:

1. Draft  
2. Pricing Review  
3. Internal Approval  
4. Proposal Delivery  
5. Customer Review  
6. Negotiation  
7. Contract Finalization  
8. Signature  
9. Closed Won  
10. Revenue Recognition

Every transition shall be fully auditable.

---

## **AI Deal Intelligence**

Artificial Intelligence shall provide:

* Deal Risk Analysis  
* Discount Recommendations  
* Margin Analysis  
* Approval Recommendations  
* Revenue Impact  
* Renewal Probability  
* Upsell Opportunities

---

## **Deal Analytics**

The platform shall measure:

* Average Deal Size  
* Win Rate  
* Discount Trends  
* Margin Performance  
* Sales Cycle Duration  
* Deal Velocity  
* Revenue Contribution  
* Executive Performance
