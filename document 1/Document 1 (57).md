# **11\. Enterprise Customer Journey Management**

## **Purpose**

The Enterprise Customer Journey Management Platform enables Tamil Business Tribe (TBT) to design, manage, monitor, optimize, and continuously improve every customer interaction throughout the complete customer lifecycle.

Rather than treating customer interactions as isolated events, the platform shall connect every touchpoint into a unified end-to-end journey.

Each journey shall be measurable, personalized, AI-assisted, and continuously optimized.

---

## **Business Objectives**

The platform shall enable TBT to:

* Understand customer behavior.  
* Visualize customer journeys.  
* Identify customer pain points.  
* Improve customer engagement.  
* Reduce customer effort.  
* Increase activation rates.  
* Improve product adoption.  
* Increase customer retention.  
* Improve conversion rates.  
* Increase customer lifetime value.  
* Reduce customer churn.  
* Improve customer satisfaction.

---

## **Journey Categories**

The platform shall support configurable journey types including:

* Visitor Journey  
* Lead Journey  
* Trial Journey  
* Customer Onboarding Journey  
* Membership Journey  
* Product Adoption Journey  
* Learning Journey  
* Community Journey  
* Support Journey  
* Renewal Journey  
* Upgrade Journey  
* Referral Journey  
* Re-engagement Journey  
* Win-back Journey

Administrators shall be able to create additional journey types without software modifications.

---

## **Journey Lifecycle**

Each customer journey shall progress through:

1. Created  
2. Draft  
3. Under Review  
4. Approved  
5. Published  
6. Active  
7. Optimized  
8. Deprecated  
9. Archived

Every journey revision shall maintain complete version history and audit records.
