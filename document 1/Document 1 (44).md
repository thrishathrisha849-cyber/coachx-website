# **36\. Enterprise Customer Advocacy Platform**

## **Purpose**

The Customer Advocacy Platform identifies highly satisfied and loyal customers who are willing to support, recommend, and publicly represent Tamil Business Tribe.

The platform shall convert positive customer experiences into measurable advocacy activities such as referrals, testimonials, reviews, case studies, event participation, and community leadership.

---

## **Business Objectives**

The platform shall help TBT to:

* Increase customer referrals.  
* Generate authentic testimonials.  
* Improve public brand trust.  
* Build long-term customer relationships.  
* Identify loyal community members.  
* Increase positive reviews.  
* Create customer success stories.  
* Develop future brand ambassadors.

---

## **Advocacy Activities**

The platform shall support:

* Customer Testimonials  
* Video Testimonials  
* Written Reviews  
* Case Studies  
* Social Media Mentions  
* Referral Campaigns  
* Event Speaker Invitations  
* Community Mentorship  
* Product Beta Testing  
* Success Story Interviews

---

## **Advocacy Readiness Score**

Every eligible customer shall receive an Advocacy Readiness Score.

The score may be calculated using:

* NPS Category  
* CSAT History  
* Membership Duration  
* Community Engagement  
* Course Completion  
* Referral History  
* Review Activity  
* Support Satisfaction  
* Brand Sentiment  
* Customer Lifetime Value

---

## **Advocacy Status**

Supported statuses include:

* Not Eligible  
* Potential Advocate  
* Advocate Ready  
* Invitation Sent  
* Active Advocate  
* Brand Ambassador  
* Temporarily Inactive  
* Removed

The platform shall maintain a full status history for audit and reporting.

---

# **37\. Enterprise Referral Intelligence Platform**

## **Purpose**

The Referral Intelligence Platform identifies customers most likely to refer others and helps the business launch personalized referral campaigns.

---

## **Referral Propensity Score**

Each customer shall receive a Referral Propensity Score based on:

* Satisfaction Level  
* NPS Response  
* Purchase Frequency  
* Membership Renewal  
* Community Activity  
* Previous Referral Success  
* Social Engagement  
* Relationship Strength

The score shall help marketing teams prioritize customers with the highest referral potential.

---

## **Referral Workflow**

The standard referral journey shall include:

1. Eligible customer identified.  
2. Referral invitation created.  
3. Personalized referral link generated.  
4. Customer shares the link.  
5. Referral visitor registers.  
6. Qualification rules are checked.  
7. Referral is approved.  
8. Reward is issued.  
9. Advocate is notified.  
10. Referral analytics are updated.

---

## **Referral Campaign Types**

The platform shall support:

* Member Refer-a-Friend  
* Course Referral  
* Event Referral  
* Community Referral  
* Membership Upgrade Referral  
* Partner Referral  
* Influencer Referral  
* Limited-Time Referral Campaign

---

## **Referral Rewards**

Possible rewards include:

* TBT Points  
* Membership Discounts  
* Free Course Access  
* Event Tickets  
* Digital Certificates  
* Merchandise  
* Account Credits  
* Exclusive Community Access

All rewards shall follow configurable eligibility and approval rules.

---

## **Referral Fraud Prevention**

The platform shall detect:

* Self-referrals  
* Duplicate accounts  
* Suspicious devices  
* Repeated IP addresses  
* Fake registrations  
* Reward abuse  
* Referral loops  
* Unusual conversion patterns

Suspicious referrals shall be placed under manual review.

---

# **38\. Enterprise Brand Ambassador Program**

## **Purpose**

The Brand Ambassador Program enables TBT to create, manage, and reward a structured network of trusted customers who actively promote the brand.

---

## **Ambassador Eligibility**

Customers may become ambassadors based on:

* Strong customer satisfaction  
* High advocacy score  
* Consistent community participation  
* Positive public reputation  
* Referral performance  
* Brand alignment  
* Content quality  
* Professional conduct

Final approval may require administrator review.

---

## **Ambassador Levels**

The platform may support:

* Community Advocate  
* Bronze Ambassador  
* Silver Ambassador  
* Gold Ambassador  
* Elite Ambassador

Each level may have different targets, permissions, rewards, and benefits.

---

## **Ambassador Responsibilities**

Ambassadors may:

* Refer new members.  
* Share approved brand content.  
* Host local or online meetups.  
* Support new community members.  
* Participate in product feedback programs.  
* Create educational content.  
* Represent TBT at selected events.  
* Provide testimonials and case studies.

---

## **Ambassador Benefits**

Benefits may include:

* Exclusive badges  
* Early feature access  
* Special training  
* Event invitations  
* Membership benefits  
* Referral incentives  
* Public profile recognition  
* Leadership opportunities  
* Dedicated support

---

## **Ambassador Dashboard**

Each ambassador shall have access to:

* Referral Count  
* Conversion Count  
* Reward Balance  
* Campaign Performance  
* Assigned Activities  
* Content Resources  
* Upcoming Events  
* Level Progress  
* Performance Score  
* Compliance Status

---

## **Ambassador Governance**

The platform shall maintain:

* Code of Conduct  
* Brand Usage Guidelines  
* Content Approval Rules  
* Confidentiality Requirements  
* Conflict of Interest Rules  
* Suspension Process  
* Removal Process  
* Audit History

# **39\. Enterprise Reputation Analytics Platform**

## **Purpose**

The Reputation Analytics Platform measures how customers and the public perceive Tamil Business Tribe across internal and external channels.

---

## **Reputation Sources**

The system may collect data from:

* TBT Reviews  
* Community Feedback  
* Course Ratings  
* Event Ratings  
* Support Feedback  
* App Store Reviews  
* Google Reviews  
* Social Media Mentions  
* Public Forums  
* Partner Feedback

External data collection shall comply with platform terms and privacy requirements.

---

## **Reputation Metrics**

The platform shall calculate:

* Overall Reputation Score  
* Average Public Rating  
* Positive Mention Rate  
* Negative Mention Rate  
* Review Volume  
* Response Rate  
* Resolution Rate  
* Brand Sentiment Index  
* Reputation Trend  
* Competitor Comparison

---

## **Review Management**

Administrators shall be able to:

* View reviews.  
* Filter reviews.  
* Assign review owners.  
* Respond to reviews.  
* Escalate critical reviews.  
* Track response status.  
* Mark reviews as resolved.  
* Report abusive content.  
* Request manual moderation.

---

## **Reputation Risk Alerts**

The system shall generate alerts when:

* Negative reviews increase rapidly.  
* A critical public complaint appears.  
* Brand sentiment declines.  
* Unanswered reviews cross a threshold.  
* A high-value customer posts negative feedback.  
* A potential viral issue is detected.

---

## **Reputation Recovery Workflow**

A reputation recovery process may include:

1. Risk detected.  
2. Issue classified.  
3. Responsible owner assigned.  
4. Customer contacted.  
5. Resolution provided.  
6. Public response approved.  
7. Follow-up completed.  
8. Reputation impact measured.

---

# **40\. Customer Success Integration**

## **Purpose**

The VoC Platform shall integrate directly with the Customer Success Platform so that customer feedback results in timely human action.

---

## **Customer Profile Integration**

The unified customer profile shall display:

* Latest Feedback  
* Sentiment History  
* Emotion History  
* CSAT Score  
* NPS Category  
* CES Score  
* Complaint History  
* Advocacy Score  
* Referral Potential  
* Reputation Risk  
* Recommended Next Action

---

## **Automatic Customer Success Actions**

The system may automatically:

* Create a follow-up task.  
* Assign a Customer Success Manager.  
* Schedule a customer call.  
* Send a personalized message.  
* Escalate a high-risk complaint.  
* Invite a promoter to an advocacy campaign.  
* Launch a recovery journey.  
* Add a customer to a retention workflow.

---

## **Customer Success Workbench**

Customer Success teams shall have access to a unified workbench containing:

* Priority Customers  
* Detractor List  
* Unresolved Complaints  
* High-Risk Accounts  
* Advocacy Opportunities  
* Follow-Up Tasks  
* Recommended Actions  
* Customer Timeline  
* Resolution Status

---

## **Closed-Loop Feedback Management**

The platform shall ensure that important feedback is acknowledged and resolved.

The closed-loop process shall include:

1. Feedback submitted.  
2. Issue analyzed.  
3. Owner assigned.  
4. Customer contacted.  
5. Action completed.  
6. Customer informed.  
7. Satisfaction rechecked.  
8. Final outcome recorded.

No critical complaint shall be closed without a documented resolution or approved exception.

# **42\. Enterprise Acceptance Criteria**

## **Feedback Collection**

* The system shall collect feedback from all configured channels.  
* Every feedback record shall contain a source, timestamp, and customer reference where available.  
* Duplicate submissions shall be identified.  
* Invalid or malicious submissions shall be flagged.

---

## **Survey Management**

* Administrators shall create and publish surveys without code changes.  
* Conditional logic shall work correctly.  
* Survey responses shall be stored securely.  
* Survey results shall be available for reporting and export.

---

## **AI and NLP Processing**

* Supported feedback shall be processed for language, sentiment, emotion, intent, themes, and categories.  
* Every AI output shall include a confidence score.  
* Low-confidence predictions shall support manual review.  
* AI model versions shall be recorded.

---

## **Complaint Management**

* Critical complaints shall generate alerts.  
* Complaints shall be routed to the correct team.  
* Escalation history shall be auditable.  
* Resolution status shall remain visible to authorized users.

---

## **Experience Intelligence**

* Experience scores shall be available by product, journey, and customer segment.  
* Trend changes shall be calculated accurately.  
* Root-cause insights shall include supporting evidence.  
* Predictive insights shall include confidence levels.

---

## **Customer Advocacy**

* Eligible advocates shall be identified using configurable rules.  
* Advocacy invitations and responses shall be tracked.  
* Testimonials and case studies shall require approval before publication.  
* Advocate consent shall be recorded.

---

## **Referral Intelligence**

* Unique referral links shall be generated correctly.  
* Referral conversions shall be attributed accurately.  
* Rewards shall only be issued after qualification.  
* Fraudulent referrals shall be flagged.

---

## **Brand Ambassador Program**

* Ambassador applications and approvals shall be tracked.  
* Ambassador levels and benefits shall be configurable.  
* Performance shall be measurable.  
* Suspended ambassadors shall lose restricted permissions immediately.

---

## **Reputation Analytics**

* Internal and supported external reviews shall be consolidated.  
* Reputation alerts shall trigger based on configurable thresholds.  
* Review responses shall have owner and status tracking.  
* Reputation trends shall be visible in dashboards.

---

## **Customer Success Integration**

* High-risk feedback shall create actionable Customer Success tasks.  
* Customer profiles shall show the latest experience intelligence.  
* Closed-loop feedback workflows shall be measurable.  
* Every completed action shall maintain an audit trail.

---

## **Security and Governance**

* Access shall follow role-based permissions.  
* Sensitive customer data shall be protected.  
* Consent requirements shall be enforced.  
* Data retention rules shall be configurable.  
* Administrative actions shall be logged.

---

## **Performance**

* Standard feedback submissions shall complete without noticeable delay.  
* Dashboards shall load within the approved enterprise performance threshold.  
* High-priority alerts shall be generated near real time.  
* Bulk analytics shall process without blocking live customer activity.

---

## **Reporting**

* Authorized users shall filter reports by date, channel, segment, product, and sentiment.  
* Reports shall support approved export formats.  
* Scheduled reports shall be delivered to configured recipients.  
* Report data shall match source records and dashboard totals.
