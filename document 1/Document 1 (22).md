# **1\. Purpose**

The Landing Pages, Forms & Lead Capture System is the primary lead acquisition engine of the Tamil Business Tribe (TBT) Marketing Automation Platform. It enables marketing teams to build high-converting landing pages, dynamic forms, lead magnets, and conversion funnels without writing code while integrating seamlessly with campaigns, workflows, CRM, Customer Data Platform (CDP), analytics, and AI services.

The platform must support millions of visitors, enterprise-grade scalability, and real-time lead processing.

---

# **2\. Objectives**

The module shall:

* Build responsive landing pages without coding.  
* Increase visitor-to-lead conversion.  
* Capture qualified leads.  
* Integrate with every marketing campaign.  
* Support AI-powered optimization.  
* Enable A/B testing.  
* Reduce form abandonment.  
* Synchronize captured leads instantly.  
* Track the complete customer acquisition funnel.
