# **21\. Enterprise Market Research Management Platform**

## **Purpose**

The Enterprise Market Research Management Platform provides a centralized environment for planning, executing, monitoring, and analyzing all research activities conducted by Tamil Business Tribe.

The platform enables research teams to manage the complete research lifecycle—from identifying a business problem to generating strategic recommendations.

It shall support both qualitative and quantitative research while maintaining enterprise governance, security, traceability, and collaboration.

---

## **Business Objectives**

The platform shall enable TBT to:

* Conduct structured market research.  
* Understand customer needs and expectations.  
* Validate product ideas before development.  
* Measure market demand.  
* Support pricing decisions.  
* Evaluate business opportunities.  
* Improve product-market fit.  
* Reduce strategic uncertainty.  
* Build a centralized research knowledge base.  
* Enable evidence-based business decisions.

---

## **Research Types**

The platform shall support:

* Market Research  
* Customer Research  
* Product Research  
* Brand Research  
* Competitor Research  
* Pricing Research  
* UX Research  
* Community Research  
* Membership Research  
* Event Research  
* Course Research  
* AI Feature Research

---

## **Research Lifecycle**

Every research project shall follow a standardized lifecycle:

1. Research Request  
2. Objective Definition  
3. Research Design  
4. Approval  
5. Data Collection  
6. Analysis  
7. Insight Generation  
8. Recommendation  
9. Executive Review  
10. Business Action  
11. Outcome Measurement  
12. Research Archive

---

# **22\. Primary Research Platform**

## **Purpose**

The Primary Research Platform enables Tamil Business Tribe to collect first-hand information directly from customers, prospects, partners, and other stakeholders.

Primary research shall generate original data owned by TBT.

---

## **Supported Research Methods**

The platform shall support:

* Customer Interviews  
* Surveys  
* Focus Groups

The Secondary Research Platform enables TBT to organize and analyze publicly available information from trusted external sources.

# **28\. Research Governance**

## **Purpose**

Research Governance ensures that all research activities conducted within Tamil Business Tribe follow standardized processes, ethical practices, and enterprise compliance requirements.

---

## **Governance Policies**

The platform shall enforce:

* Research Approval Workflow  
* Data Quality Standards  
* Participant Consent Management  
* Privacy Compliance  
* Document Version Control  
* Access Control  
* Review and Approval  
* Retention Policies  
* Audit Logging  
* Regulatory Compliance

---

## **Research Roles**

The platform shall support:

* Research Administrator  
* Research Manager  
* Research Analyst  
* Product Manager  
* Marketing Manager  
* Executive Reviewer  
* Legal Reviewer  
* External Research Partner (Restricted)

Each role shall have configurable permissions.

---

## **Audit Requirements**

Every research activity shall record:

* User  
* Action  
* Timestamp  
* Previous Value  
* Updated Value  
* Approval Status  
* Review Comments  
* Version Number

No approved research document shall be permanently deleted. Archived versions shall remain available to authorized users for audit and historical reference.
