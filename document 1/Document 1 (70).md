# **31\. Enterprise Partner Portal**

## **Purpose**

The Enterprise Partner Portal shall serve as the unified digital workspace for every registered partner within the Tamil Business Tribe (TBT) ecosystem.

The portal shall provide secure, role-based access to all partner operations including sales, marketing, learning, certifications, incentives, opportunities, support, analytics, community engagement, AI-powered recommendations, and executive communications.

The Partner Portal shall become the primary collaboration platform between TBT and its partner ecosystem.

---

## **Business Objectives**

The platform shall enable partners to:

* Manage business opportunities.  
* Track sales pipeline.  
* Register deals.  
* View commissions.  
* Access marketing resources.  
* Complete certifications.  
* Submit support requests.  
* Participate in partner communities.  
* Monitor performance.  
* Receive AI-powered business recommendations.

---

## **Portal Modules**

The Partner Portal shall include:

* Dashboard  
* Partner Profile  
* Sales Workspace  
* Opportunity Management  
* Deal Registration  
* Lead Center

* Commission Center  
* Learning Center

* Community  
* Support Center  
* Notifications  
* Reports

Each module shall support responsive web and mobile experiences.

# **38\. Enterprise Acceptance Criteria**

## **Partner Operations**

* Every partner shall progress through configurable lifecycle workflows.  
* Registration, verification, onboarding, and certification shall be fully auditable.  
* Partner profiles shall maintain a unified 360° enterprise view.  
* Portal access shall follow configurable role-based permissions.

---

## **Channel & Affiliate Operations**

* Channel opportunities shall support collaborative selling workflows.  
* Deal registration shall prevent duplicate submissions and channel conflicts.  
* Affiliate tracking shall accurately attribute referrals and conversions.  
* Commission calculations shall support configurable business rules and approval workflows.

---

## **Learning & Enablement**

* Learning paths shall support self-paced and instructor-led training.  
* Certifications shall include renewal workflows and validity tracking.  
* Marketing resources shall maintain version control and approval history.  
* Community participation shall be moderated through configurable governance policies.

---

## **AI Intelligence**

* AI shall provide recommendations for partner growth, channel optimization, affiliate performance, and revenue opportunities.  
* AI-generated insights shall include confidence scores and supporting rationale.  
* AI recommendations shall remain advisory unless explicitly approved through business workflows.  
* AI activities shall be fully logged for audit purposes.

---

## **Governance & Security**

* Every commercial and operational event shall maintain immutable audit logs.  
* Sensitive partner information shall be encrypted in transit and at rest.  
* RBAC, MFA, and SSO shall be supported across the Partner Portal.  
* Executive dashboards shall provide real-time visibility into partner ecosystem performance and compliance.

---

## **Performance & Scalability**

* The platform shall support millions of partner organizations, affiliates, referrals, opportunities, and transactions.  
* Analytics, AI processing, and reporting shall operate independently from transactional workloads.  
* The architecture shall support multi-region, multi-language, multi-currency, and multi-tenant deployments.  
* The platform shall be extensible to support future partner programs, marketplace integrations, and ecosystem innovations.
