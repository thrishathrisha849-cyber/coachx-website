# **1\. Purpose**

The Enterprise Customer Segmentation & Audience Intelligence System shall enable Tamil Business Tribe to automatically classify every customer into meaningful, AI-powered audience segments based on demographics, behavior, interests, engagement, purchasing patterns, learning activity, community participation and predictive analytics.

The platform shall continuously update audience segments in real time so every marketing campaign, recommendation, notification and personalization experience targets the right customer at the right moment. Modern enterprise segmentation combines demographic, psychographic and behavioral data to improve personalization and campaign performance.

---

# **2\. Vision**

The platform shall evolve from static customer lists into an intelligent, self-learning audience engine capable of identifying high-value customers, predicting future behavior, discovering hidden audience clusters and activating them automatically across every marketing channel. AI-assisted segmentation can continuously refine audiences beyond manually maintained rules.
