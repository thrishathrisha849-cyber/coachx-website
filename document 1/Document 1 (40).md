# **2\. Purpose**

The Enterprise Voice of Customer (VoC) Platform enables Tamil Business Tribe to continuously collect, analyze, understand, and act upon customer feedback across every interaction.

The platform transforms raw feedback into measurable business intelligence, helping every department improve customer satisfaction, engagement, retention, and product quality.

---

# **3\. Vision**

Create a world-class Voice of Customer ecosystem where every customer interaction becomes actionable intelligence.

The platform should:

* Listen to every customer  
* Understand customer intent  
* Measure customer experience  
* Detect satisfaction trends  
* Identify friction points  
* Recommend improvements  
* Drive customer advocacy  
* Continuously improve products and services

---

# **4\. Business Objectives**

The platform shall enable the organization to:

* Increase Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)  
* Improve Net Promoter Score (NPS)  
* Reduce Customer Effort Score (CES)  
* Increase retention  
* Improve product quality  
* Reduce customer complaints  
* Increase referrals  
* Build customer loyalty  
* Strengthen brand reputation  
* Create a customer-first organization

---

# **7\. Enterprise Voice of Customer Architecture**

The platform follows a five-layer architecture.

### **Layer 1 – Feedback Sources**

Customer touchpoints including:

* Mobile App  
* Web Portal  
* Community  
* Learning Platform  
* Membership  
* Events  
* Support  
* Live Chat  
* Email  
* WhatsApp  
* Push Notifications  
* Surveys  
* Reviews

---

### **Layer 2 – Collection Layer**

Responsible for:

* Receiving feedback  
* Validating responses  
* Removing duplicates  
* Spam detection  
* Identity mapping  
* Session linking  
* Timestamp creation

---

### **Layer 3 – Intelligence Layer**

Processes collected feedback using:

* AI  
* NLP  
* Sentiment Analysis  
* Theme Detection  
* Classification  
* Priority Scoring

---

### **Layer 4 – Action Layer**

Automatically creates:

* Improvement Tasks  
* Product Requests  
* Support Escalations  
* Customer Success Actions  
* Marketing Campaigns  
* Executive Alerts

---

### **Layer 5 – Reporting Layer**

Provides dashboards for:

* Management  
* Marketing  
* Customer Success  
* Product Teams  
* Operations  
* Executive Leadership
