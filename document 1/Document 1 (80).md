# **11\. Enterprise Business Intelligence Management**

## **Purpose**

The Enterprise Business Intelligence Management Platform enables Tamil Business Tribe (TBT) to centrally govern, manage, monitor, and optimize all business intelligence assets across the enterprise.

The platform shall provide a unified Business Intelligence 360° Workspace that consolidates operational, financial, commercial, customer, marketing, product, partner, and AI-generated intelligence into a trusted enterprise analytics environment.

Business Intelligence Management shall become the authoritative source of truth for enterprise analytics.

---

## **Business Objectives**

The platform shall enable TBT to:

* Centralize business intelligence operations.  
* Improve enterprise data visibility.  
* Standardize analytics.  
* Improve executive reporting.  
* Enable enterprise-wide KPI governance.  
* Increase reporting automation.  
* Improve strategic planning.  
* Strengthen business decision-making.  
* Improve organizational performance.  
* Enable AI-assisted analytics.

---

## **Intelligence Domains**

The platform shall support configurable intelligence domains including:

* Executive Intelligence  
* Financial Intelligence  
* Revenue Intelligence  
* Sales Intelligence  
* Marketing Intelligence  
* Customer Intelligence  
* Customer Success Intelligence  
* Product Intelligence  
* Community Intelligence  
* Learning Intelligence  
* Operations Intelligence  
* Partner Intelligence  
* Human Resources Intelligence  
* Technology Intelligence  
* Risk Intelligence

Organizations may define additional intelligence domains without software modifications.

---

## **Business Intelligence Profile**

Each intelligence entity shall maintain:

* Intelligence ID  
* Business Unit  
* Department  
* Data Source  
* KPI Collection  
* Reporting Owner  
* Analytics Category  
* Data Refresh Schedule  
* Business Priority  
* Sensitivity Classification  
* Governance Status  
* AI Confidence Score  
* Audit History

---

## **Business Intelligence Workspace**

The unified workspace shall include:

* Executive Overview  
* KPI Library  
* Reports  
* Dashboards  
* Trend Analysis  
* Comparative Analysis  
* Predictive Analytics  
* AI Insights  
* Business Alerts  
* Strategic Initiatives  
* Benchmarking  
* Executive Notes  
* Audit Timeline
