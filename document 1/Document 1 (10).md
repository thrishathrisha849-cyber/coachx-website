# **2\. PRODUCT VISION**

Tamil Business Tribe Marketplace must become a trusted Tamil-first digital business commerce ecosystem.

The marketplace must help:

* Tamil entrepreneurs sell business resources.  
* Freelancers sell professional services.  
* Mentors sell consulting packages.  
* Creators sell ebooks, templates and digital assets.  
* Businesses find verified Tamil-speaking professionals.  
* Members purchase reliable solutions.  
* TBT generate platform revenue through commissions.  
* Sellers build reputation and recurring income.  
* Buyers receive secure delivery and support.  
* Admin teams manage quality, fraud and financial operations.

The marketplace must not operate as a simple product-listing page.

It must provide:

* Seller verification  
* Quality control  
* Secure transactions  
* Controlled fulfilment  
* Buyer protection  
* Seller protection  
* Transparent fees  
* Trusted reviews  
* Dispute resolution

* Seller verification  
* Customer support  
* Data security  
* Payment accuracy  
* Legal compliance

* Product or service  
* Seller  
* Deliverables  
* Price  
* Taxes  
* Platform fees  
* Delivery timeline  
* Licence  
* Revision limits  
* Cancellation policy  
* Refund policy  
* Support period

* Gross sale amount  
* Discount funding  
* Platform commission  
* Payment fee  
* Tax withholding  
* Refund reserve  
* Net earning  
* Payout date

## **4.3 Server-Authoritative Commerce**

The client application must never decide:

* Final price  
* Commission  
* Seller earning  
* Download permission  
* Order completion  
* Refund amount  
* Payout balance  
* Inventory availability  
* Service milestone release

## **4.4 Historical Snapshots**

Each order must preserve:

* Product title  
* Product description  
* Product price  
* Seller details  
* Licence terms  
* Refund policy  
* Deliverables  
* Commission rate  
* Tax rate  
* Delivery timeline

Future product changes must not modify past orders.

## **4.5 Controlled Marketplace Access**

Only approved sellers must be able to publish marketplace listings.

Product publication must support:

* Automated validation  
* Manual moderation  
* Legal review where needed  
* Copyright review  
* Category restrictions  
* Quality review
